#225 Juzo Naniwa | Juzo | Keaton Yamada
A Combattler Team member and pilot of Battle Crusher, Unit 2.
A cool cynic with a sharp tongue, but his Kansai dialect takes the edge off.
He sometimes clashes with Hyoma, even coming to blows, but deep down they see each other as comrades.
A marksman genius who won a contest at age 8, he often saves the team with his shooting.
#226 Daisaku Nishikawa | Daisaku | Kazuya Tatekabe
A Combattler Team member and pilot of Battle Tank, Unit 3.
Easygoing, a gentle giant.
From Kyushu, he dreams of being a manga artist and is a judo master.
#227 Chizuru Nanbara | Chizuru | Miyuki Ueda
The Combattler Team's only woman and pilot of Battle Marine, Unit 4.
Granddaughter of Dr. Nanbara, creator of the Combattler V, and the team's idol.
A gifted woman versed in oceanography, medicine and robotics, she also excels at piano, swimming and gymnastics. Kind but a tomboy. She loves Hyoma and has risked herself to protect him, but she cannot be honest, so their romance goes slowly.
#228 Kosuke Kita | Kosuke | Sachiko Chijimatsu
The Combattler Team's youngest member and pilot of Battle Craft, Unit 5.
A boy genius who studied at an American university while in grade school, making many brilliant (or silly?) inventions.
Yet he is still a child, losing his cool when the enemy targets Hokkaido, where his parents live.
His weakness: he still wets the bed.
#229 Dr. Nanbara | Nanbara
Chizuru's grandfather.
A genius scientist who foresaw the alien invasion and built the Combattler V and the Nanbara Connection to fight it, gathering the five pilots.
He died shielding Dr. Yotsuya in an enemy attack, leaving everything to him.
#230 Dr. Yotsuya | Yotsuya
The scientist who heads the Nanbara Connection after Dr. Nanbara's death.
He was Nanbara's old friend.
When Nanbara first asked him to lead the Combattler Team, he refused, bitterly saying mankind deserved to perish. But moved by Nanbara, who gave his life to save him, he resolved to fight for the nature he loves.
Bold and foul-mouthed, but indomitable, he never gives up looking for a way out.
He loves liquor and always carries a bottle of whiskey.
#231 Ropet | Ropet | Masako Nozawa
A brain wave robot built by Dr. Nanbara for the combine. It measures the five pilots' brain waves; only when the meters on its body line up, confirming they are in sync, can they combine. In short, without Ropet there is no combine. It has human-like feelings, and once fell for a pretty robot girl sent by the enemy.
#232 Garuda | Garuda | Osamu Ichikawa
Battle commander of the Campbellians.
In battle or rage he takes the form of a bird-man. He fought on the orders of his mother Oreana, supreme commander of the invasion, to remake Earth for the Campbellians.
Proud, he saw the Combattler V and Hyoma as his rivals.
But on learning that he was a sophisticated android made by Oreana, he turned on her in rage in the Big Garuda for lying to him.
After killing Oreana, he staked his pride on a final duel with the Combattler V.
#233 Oreana | Oreana | Masako Nozawa
Commander of the Campbellian invasion of Earth.
She looks like a giant stone idol, but is really a computer holding the mind of the Campbellian scientist Oreana.
She taught the android Garuda that she was his mother, binding him to total loyalty.
She forced the Combattler V apart with the Null Magne Ray when it stormed her base, but Garuda, furious at learning the truth, attacked her in the Big Garuda.
#234 Miia | Miia | Sachiko Chijimatsu
A halfroid directly under Garuda, her lower half part of a wall.
Commander of the Campbellian rest area, her main job is soothing Garuda with songs and music. An android with feelings, she risks her life to defeat the Combattler V out of love for Garuda.
#235 Kenichi Go | Kenichi | Yukinaga Shiraishi
Leader of the Voltes Team, eldest of the Go brothers. He pilots Volt Cruiser, Unit 1, and is Voltes V's main pilot.
He excels at shooting and motorcycles.
An earnest honor student with a leader's strong sense of duty and justice. But like his brothers he grows emotional about their father Kentaro, and at first often clashed with the cynical Ippei. Facing trials like his father and the secret of his birth, he supported his brothers and fought for all who wish for peace on Earth and Boazan.
#236 Ippei Mine | Ippei | Kazuyuki Sogabe
A Voltes Team member and pilot of Volt Bomber, Unit 2.
Born to immigrant parents in America, he lost his father to illness after their farm failed, and his mother died saving the family from wolves, leaving him to live alone.
His riding skills from America won him three straight national rodeo titles, and he is a master of the whip.
His past made him a loner and a cynic, and at first he often clashed with leader Kenichi, but the fighting forged a bond between them.
#237 Daijiro Go | Daijiro | Tessho Genda
A Voltes Team member and pilot of Volt Panzer, Unit 3.
Second of the Go brothers, huge and strong. A master of staff and sword, he once taught Kenichi the secret of catching a blade barehanded, called Kocho Gaeshi.
Idolizing Saigo Takamori, he alone of the brothers speaks the Kagoshima dialect.
#238 Hiyoshi Go | Hiyoshi | Noriko Ohara
The Voltes Team's youngest and pilot of Volt Frigate, Unit 4.
Youngest of the Go brothers, he barely remembers his father Kentaro.
An underwater specialist, he inherited his parents' science genes most strongly, with a genius for machines. Still young and needy, his longing for his father led him into enemy traps.
#239 Megumi Oka | Megumi | Miyuki Ueda
The Voltes Team's only woman and pilot of Volt Lander, Unit 5.
The 18th-generation kunoichi of the Koga ninja, and daughter of General Oka.
Tomboyish, yet also gentle and devoted to her father. Refereeing fights between Kenichi and Ippei is her job.
Skilled at intelligence, she is at home on infiltration missions.
#240 General Oka | Oka
Supreme commander of the Earth Defense Force.
Big Falcon, Voltes V's base, was built on the plans of four people: Oka, Dr. Hamaguchi and the Go couple.
The 17th head of the Koga ninja, and father of Megumi Oka.
He gave his life in battle protecting Megumi.
#241 Dr. Hamaguchi | Hamaguchi
Director of Big Falcon, the Voltes Team's base. An authority on astronomy and physics, and Mitsuyo's old college professor.
Believing the Go couple, he helped defend Earth and led Big Falcon's construction. At times stern, at times loving with the team, he supported them as more than a base commander.
He died ramming an enemy beast fighter to save Voltes V.
#242 Kentaro Go | Kentaro Go
Father of the Go brothers.
He is really La Gour, a Boazanian and nephew of the former emperor, who nearly took the throne.
Though royal, he had love for all people alike, and questioned Boazan's unjust custom of ranking people by their horns. He was also a first-rate scientist. Hunted for opposing Boazan's discrimination, he fled to Earth, met and married Mitsuyo Go, and took the name Kentaro Go.
Foreseeing that his homeworld would invade Earth, he built Voltes V and Big Falcon with Dr. Hamaguchi and others, then went back to Boazan to change it.
He meets his sons again amid the war between Boazan and Earth.
#243 Mitsuyo Go | Mitsuyo
Mother of the Go brothers.
She helped La Gour when he reached Earth; they fell in love and married.
She worked with her renamed husband Kentaro and her mentor Dr. Hamaguchi to build Voltes V and Big Falcon. After Kentaro returned to Boazan, she trained her sons hard as Voltes V pilots for the coming invasion. She died ramming an enemy beast fighter to save Voltes V.
#244 Prince Heinel | Heinel | Osamu Ichikawa
Commander of Boazan's Earth invasion force.
Son of La Gour, branded a traitor, he was abused as a child for it.
To clear his name as a traitor's son, he studied war and strategy seeking glory, and finally became commander of the invasion force.
With a warrior's pride and a commander's cunning, he shows no mercy to enemies, traitors or cowards.
But he learns he was a tool of others' ambitions and is torn.
After the final battle with Voltes V, he learns the secret of his birth, and stunned by all he has done, vanishes into the flames.
#245 Do Zuhl | Do Zuhl | Kan Tokumaru
A Boazan noble and one of the invasion force's generals. A sly petty villain who puts himself first.
He is really a spy sent by Emperor Zu Zambajil to watch Heinel. So he flatters Heinel to his face and curses him behind his back.
Heinel finally unmasks him, uses him, and has him executed.
#246 Rui Jangal | Jangal | Shozo Iizuka
One of the generals of Boazan's invasion force.
Heinel's right-hand man, utterly loyal to him.
A field commander who goes to battle in the Skullrook and gives orders to the beast fighters.
A pure warrior, as bold as he looks, but quick to anger.
Refusing to surrender, he takes his own life.
#247 Katherine Rii | Katherine
One of the generals of Boazan's invasion force.
A brilliant biologist, she raises and selects creatures to make into beast fighters. Beautiful but cold in her work. She secretly loves Heinel, with whom she grew up, and works to protect him after he loses his command, but dies shielding him.
#248 Shinobu Fujiwara | Shinobu | Kazuki Yao
Leader of the Cyber Beast Force.
A born dogfighter, as if made to fly his Eagle Fighter. Quick to fight, he picks on anyone he dislikes. But having no hidden side, once he opens up he is a true friend.
A soldier who talks back to superiors and breaks orders at will.
In Dancouga he says he enlisted over heartbreak, youthful setbacks and rebellion against his dad. He and teammate Sara fight constantly, but they care for each other.
His catchphrase: "Let's do this!"
#249 Sara Yuki | Sara | Yuriko Yamamoto
A member of the Cyber Beast Force.
She pilots the Land Cougar.
Shinobu's academy classmate, she was the lover of their instructor Shapiro.
But Shapiro defected to the Muge Zorbados Empire, and Shinobu stopped Sara from going with him.
She fought Shapiro with mixed love and hate, and finally defeated him herself, breaking with her past for good.
As fiery as the rest of the team, her hand is often faster than her mouth. She and Shinobu care for each other, and they confirm their feelings as the story goes on.
#250 Ryo Shiba | Ryo | Kaneto Shiozawa
A member of the Cyber Beast Force and pilot of the Big Moth.
The calmest member, with superb judgment. But he is a bit sarcastic, and often clashed with the impulsive Shinobu.
Still, his advice is usually right, making him the team's anchor.
A master of Chinese kenpo, he puts it to use piloting his beast machine.
He later proposes to and marries Daniela, a girl he met in the war.
#251 Masato Shikibu | Masato | Shigeru Nakahara
The Cyber Beast Force's youngest member and pilot of the Land Liger.
A cheerful joker and the team's mood maker, but in battle he is as fierce as the rest.
His father runs a defense company, and he enlisted to rebel against him.
A ladies' man, he fell for Laura, a war orphan Shinobu took in, and wooed her.
He says he does better in real battle than in practice.
His baseball skills are said to amaze even married women.
#252 Alan Igor | Alan | Hideyuki Tanaka
Son of Commander Igor, also called the Black Knight. Rebelling against his father, he formed a guerrilla unit, the "Black Knights," fighting Muge Zorbados on his own and often saving Shinobu's team. He loses his father in battle just after they reconcile, then fully backs the team in his stead.
In the battle with Muge on the Moon, he storms a giant enemy ship alone, trading his life to take the lunar base.
#253 Ross Igor | Igor
Commander of the Cyber Beast Force.
Strict and mission-first, Shinobu's team often rebels, but his words carry weight, and deep down they trust him fully. Estranged from his son Alan over their differences, he reconciles with him as he dies defending the base, leaving him his cause.
#254 Kotaro Hazuki | Hazuki
An authority on bio-high-tech and creator of the beast machines.
At first puzzled by Shinobu's team's wildness, he came to understand them well.
He seems cold, but he is humane, taking in the war orphan Laura. He is also gutsy, fighting back with a machine gun when the enemy stormed the base. Once deputy commander, he took command of the Cyber Beast Force after Igor died.
#255 Grawl | Grawl
The Cyber Beast Force's transport pilot.
Not an academy man, he rose from the ranks to lead the transport team.
Friendly and down-to-earth, Shinobu's team looked up to him as a big brother. When Shapiro's deadly encirclement trapped them, he drew the enemy horde away as a decoy in his transport and was killed.
His sacrifice opened the way for Dancouga's first combination.
#256 Shapiro Keats | Shapiro | Norio Wakamoto
An instructor at Shinobu's academy and Sara's former lover.
Calm and a brilliant strategist and tactician.
He warned his superiors to prepare for an alien attack, but was ignored, and when aliens came, the military's weakness disgusted him. He gave up on Earth and defected to the alien Muge Zorbados Empire. Made Muge's strategist, he leaked Earth's secrets and exploited human weakness to torment the Cyber Beast Force, but repeated defeats cost him his place in Muge too.
In a final battle to regain his standing, he is slain by his ex-lover Sara, dying in ambition and madness.
#257 Akira Hibiki | Akira | Akira Kamiya
A sporty boy, captain of the Rinkai Academy soccer team.
Son of Ichiro, a Mutron researcher, and Princess Lemuria of the Mu Empire, he is destined to fuse ("fade in") with Raideen, legacy of King La Mu.
He fights the Demon Empire in Raideen to stop the coming Demon Century.
A cheerful, hot-blooded hero, strong enough to face his fate and brave even when all seems lost.
But longing for his mother Lemuria, who was still alive, he sometimes lost his cool.
