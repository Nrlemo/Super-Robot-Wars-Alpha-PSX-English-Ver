#241 Oberius

#242 Zugal
A demon-type Mycenae Battle Beast. It appears in episode 3, "Burning Guts!! The Wild Venus!!", the Venus A's debut. Its body and head can split and act apart.
#243 Arsos
A superhuman-type Mycenae Battle Beast.
Part of the Mycenae vanguard in the movie "Mazinger Z vs. the Great General of Darkness." Its spear nearly destroys Mazinger Z. It is also the foe Mazinger Z fought with Great Mazinger's borrowed Mazinger Blade.
#244 Dante
A demon-type Mycenae Battle Beast.
Part of the Mycenae vanguard in the movie "Mazinger Z vs. the Great General of Darkness," it nearly destroys the Photon Power Laboratory. It speaks intelligently, showing that Battle Beasts are transformed survivors of ancient Mycenae. So when Koji first saw Dante, he mistook the Battle Beasts for talking Mechanical Beasts.
#245 Jaraga
A reptile-type Mycenae Battle Beast.
It appears with other Battle Beasts in the Mycenae vanguard in the Mazinger movies, attacking Tokyo and fighting Mazinger Z.
#246 Great General of Darkness
A general of an enemy of Mycenae, revived as a humanoid Battle Beast after losing to the Emperor of Darkness, Mycenae's ruler.
Now the Emperor's loyal servant, he led seven Battle Beast legions to conquer the surface. A brave warrior, he fell in a duel with Great Mazinger. His grieving men showed how they trusted him, proof of a true general.
#247 Mikeros
A flying fortress of the Mycenae Empire.
At home on land, sea and air, it carries the seven legions' generals and Battle Beasts to the front. Weapons are built into the faces on its four sides.
#248 Mass Produced Great Mazinger
A mass-produced Great Mazinger from Gosaku Ota's manga version.
Shinsumi Heavy Industries, maker of the humanoid weapon GMFA1, got Great Mazinger's plans illegally and built it. It looks and performs basically like the original, but has a number on its chest. In the manga, to tell them apart, Boss painted "The Original" on the waist of Tetsuya's Great Mazinger. There is also one all-black mass-produced Great Mazinger.
#249 Gilgilgan
Appears in the movie "Great Mazinger vs. Getter Robo."
A space monster sent by a mysterious saucer to invade Earth. Hatched from an egg, it grows by eating metal, troubling Great Mazinger and Getter Robo. From a lizard with insect legs, to a second form sprouting a humanoid torso, it took in the saucer's energy to reach this final humanoid form. Every form has very hard armor, but its weak point is the hole left when it removes its final form's weapon, the scythe at its waist.
#250 Mecha Gilgilgan
An evolved form of the space monster Gilgilgan.
It fuses the metal it has eaten at the cellular level, metalizing its whole body and using all its stored energy. In this form its defense and power rise sharply. The hole at the waist of the third form is also sealed, leaving no real weak point. Mecha Gilgilgan is not in the Mazinger movies; it was created for Super Robot Wars.
#251 Dragonsaurus
Appears in the movie "Grendizer, Getter Robo G, Great Mazinger: Decisive Battle! The Great Sea Monster." A giant sea monster, the ancient marine beast Dragonsaurus mutated by absorbing heavy oil. Its eight heads regrow in an instant when cut, and its giant body's immense power troubled the super robots, including Great Mazinger and Getter Robo G. Its soft body also absorbs the impact of solid weapons. The oil it absorbed is stored in its body, so firing on it is its weak point. It was Boss, swallowed by the Dragonsaurus, who realized this.
#252 Pikudron
Appears in the movie "Great Mazinger vs. Getter Robo G: Clash in the Sky." An invader robot sent by mysterious aliens. Wrapped in light, it is also called the "light wave beast." It looks organic, but a mechanical body lies beneath its surface of light. The light is a strong barrier that repels physical attacks and absorbs beams, troubling Great Mazinger and Getter Robo G. "Clash in the Sky" is also where Getter Robo G and Great Mazinger's Great Booster are completed.
#253 Getter 1
A combining super robot formed by three fighters, built by Dr. Saotome.
Meant for space development, it is armed to face the Dinosaur Empire of Emperor Gore. It runs on Getter Rays, with armor of Getter alloy.
Getter 1 forms with the Eagle, Jaguar and Bear linking from the head down. It flies at Mach 2 with its Getter Wing and is used mainly for aerial combat. The strongest of Getter Robo's three forms overall, and the most used. The Missile Machine Gun used by Getter 1 in the game comes from Ken Ishikawa's manga.
#254 Getter 2
A combining super robot formed by three fighters, built by Dr. Saotome.
Meant for space development, it is armed to face the Dinosaur Empire of Emperor Gore. It runs on Getter Rays, with armor of Getter alloy. Getter 2 forms with the Jaguar, Bear and Eagle linking from the head down, for ground and underground combat. Its greatest weapons, besides the drill arm on its left, are its speed: it runs at Mach 3 and uses the Getter Vision, a clone trick that dazzles foes with afterimages.
It can also burrow underground with its drill arm.
#255 Getter 3
A combining super robot formed by three fighters, built by Dr. Saotome.
Meant for space development, it is armed to face the Dinosaur Empire of Emperor Gore. It runs on Getter Rays, with armor of Getter alloy. Getter 3 forms with the Bear, Eagle and Jaguar from the head down; unlike the others it has treads for legs, and fights mainly underwater. The strongest form, it also uses pilot Musashi's judo throw, the Daisetsuzan Oroshi.
#256 Eagle
One of Getter Robo's Get Machines. Combined, it forms Getter 1's head and chest, Getter 2's legs and Getter 3's torso. Known for its agility.
#257 Jaguar
One of Getter Robo's Get Machines. Combined, it forms Getter 2's head and chest, Getter 1's torso and Getter 3's treads. Aerodynamic.
#258 Bear
One of Getter Robo's Get Machines. Combined, it forms Getter 3's head, Getter 1's legs and Getter 2's torso. Very stable.
#259 Getter Dragon
A new Getter Robo built by Dr. Saotome for combat from the start.
A Getter Ray amplifier gives it 10 times the power of the first Getter, with armor of the new Synthetic Steel G. In the anime it fought the Hyakki Empire of Emperor Burai.
Getter Dragon forms with the Dragon, Liger and Poseidon in that order, mainly for aerial combat, piloted by Ryoma as with Getter 1. Like Getter 1, it is the strongest of its three forms, and its finest finisher is the Shine Spark, added midway through Getter Robo G. The Getter Laser Cannon used in the game comes from Ken Ishikawa's manga.
#260 Getter Liger
A new Getter Robo built by Dr. Saotome for combat from the start.
A Getter Ray amplifier gives it 10 times the power of the first Getter, with armor of the new Synthetic Steel G. Getter Liger forms with the Liger, Poseidon and Dragon in that order, mainly for ground and underground combat, piloted by Hayato as with Getter 2. Like Getter 2, it is the fastest form, using the Mach Special clone trick. Its right hand is a five-fingered manipulator that turns into a drill as needed. In the anime, Getter Liger could fly from the start.
#261 Getter Poseidon
A new Getter Robo built by Dr. Saotome for combat from the start.
A Getter Ray amplifier gives it 10 times the power of the first Getter, with armor of the new Synthetic Steel G. Getter Poseidon forms with the Poseidon, Dragon and Liger in that order; unlike Getter 3 it walks on two legs, and is used mainly underwater. Its lower legs fold into treads.
It is piloted by Benkei, who joined the Getter Team in place of Musashi, killed fighting the Dinosaur Empire. In the anime it never uses the "Daisetsuzan Oroshi" as in the game.
Like Getter 3, it is the most powerful of its three forms. Later in Getter Robo G it was modified to fly.
#262 Dragon
A Get Machine forming Getter Dragon's head and chest, Getter Liger's legs and Getter Poseidon's torso. Well balanced, with a top speed of Mach 1.
#263 Liger
A Get Machine forming Getter Liger's head and chest, Getter Poseidon's legs and Getter Dragon's torso. Built for speed, with a top speed of Mach 1.
#264 Poseidon
A Get Machine forming Getter Poseidon's head and chest, Getter Dragon's legs and Getter Liger's torso. Built for power, with a top speed of Mach 1.
#265 Shin Getter 1
The ultimate Getter Robo, built to draw out the full power of Getter Rays. It fights every foe that threatens Getter Rays and humanity. Shin Getter 1 forms with the Shin Eagle, Shin Jaguar and Shin Bear from the head down, mainly for aerial combat. Its agility allows sudden stops, starts and turns that seem to ignore inertia. But it needs vast Getter Rays to start, and even the Getter Dragon had to serve as an amplifier.
The strongest of its three forms, piloted by Ryoma as with Getter Dragon. It sometimes moves as if it had a will of its own, and much about it is a mystery. At full power Shin Getter changes forms by morphing, but normally it changes by splitting and combining Get Machines. The Getter Blast Cannon comes from Ken Ishikawa's manga. The Shin Shine Spark is a game-original finisher, though similar moves appear several times in the manga.
#266 Shin Getter 2
The ultimate Getter Robo, built to draw out the full power of Getter Rays. It fights every foe that threatens Getter Rays and humanity. Shin Getter 2 (Shin Jaguar up top, Shin Eagle below, Shin Bear on the back) fights on and under the ground. Faster than Getter Liger, it dazzles foes with the Shin Mach Special.
#267 Shin Getter 3
The ultimate Getter Robo, built to draw out the full power of Getter Rays. It fights every foe that threatens Getter Rays and humanity. Formed by the Shin Bear (upper body), Shin Jaguar (torso) and Shin Eagle (treads), it fights on land and underwater. Piloted by Benkei, as with Getter Poseidon.

#272 Getter 1
Getter 1, flown by Musashi alone.

#279 Dai
A giant battleship of the Dinosaur Empire.
Seen in episodes 50 and 51 (the finale). Carrying four Mechasaurs, 200 bombers and more, it is the Empire's last trump card. Its armor and firepower live up to the name "invincible battleship"; the Getter Robo fails its one chance, an attack from within, and is knocked out. Blaming himself, Musashi loaded Michiru's command machine with bombs and charged in, never to return...
#280 Combattler V
A combining super robot built by Dr. Nanbara with super electromagnetic physics, foreseeing an alien invasion. It forms from five Battle Machines: Battle Jet, Battle Crusher, Battle Tank, Battle Marine and Battle Craft. The combine call is "Let's Combine." It runs on super electromagnetic energy, with cermet armor. To combine, the five pilots' brain waves must sync, which the small robot Ropet must confirm. It is known for its many weapons, added several times in the anime, nearly 20 in all (some not in the game). It is also probably the robot whose height and weight the most people in Japan know by heart.
#281 Battle Jet
Battle Machine 1, forming Combattler V's head. The fastest Battle Machine, a high-performance fighter with weapons for both near and far. With Hyoma flying it, it is strong alone. In the anime a second unit was flown by Kenji Kawakami, "the Reaper."
#282 Battle Crusher
Battle Machine 2, forming Combattler V's arms and chest. Built as a heavy bomber, but in combat it mainly uses missiles, playing to Juzo's marksmanship.
#283 Battle Tank
Battle Machine 3, forming Combattler V's torso. A heavy ground tank driven by Daisaku; when combining, it is lifted into the air by powerful super electromagnetic energy. It cannot fly, so the Battle Marine carries it when it must.
#284 Battle Marine
Battle Machine 4, forming Combattler V's legs. A high-performance submarine that can also be used as a fighter. Its Mechanifixer unit makes field repairs to the Battle Machines. Piloted by Chizuru, granddaughter of its creator, Dr. Nanbara.
#285 Battle Craft
Battle Machine 5, forming Combattler V's feet. Alone, Kosuke flies it as a scout. A retractable drill lets it burrow underground, gathering data on any terrain.
