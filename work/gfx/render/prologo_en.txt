Nearly two centuries have passed since humanity expanded into space. The Earth Sphere was divided into two major powers. One is the Earth Federation Government, which unified the Earth Sphere, and the other is the group of space colonies floating in space. Originally, the space colonies were under the jurisdiction of the Earth Federation Government, but over the long years, their numbers increased, and their significance as space colonies began to fade.

In the year 179 of the New Western Calendar, Side 3, the colony cluster furthest from Earth, declared itself the "Principality of Zeon" and launched a war of independence against the Earth Federation Government. The Zeon forces, possessing humanoid mobile weapons, initially had the upper hand, but due to the fierce counterattack by the Earth Federation Forces, the war fell into a stalemate. However, just before the Earth Federation Forces could launch their final operation, "Operation Star One," a mysterious giant object appeared in the L5 airspace, swallowing the majority of both the Earth Federation and Principality of Zeon space fleets, and crashed onto South Ataria Island on Earth. Both armies, militarily exhausted, signed a ceasefire agreement triggered by this incident. Later, this conflict, known as the "One Year War," came to an end.

After the war, the EOTI Institute, an investigative body led by Dr. Bian Zoldark, determined that the object that crashed on South Ataria Island was a super-massive space battleship created by an unknown civilization. This fact proved that intelligent lifeforms exist besides humanity, and that a large-scale war was taking place outside the solar system. However, the high officials of the Earth Federation Government refused to acknowledge this reality, and for their own self-preservation, sought to strengthen their rule over the Earth Sphere and build up the Federation's military.

Under these circumstances, Dr. Bian Zoldark and others, predicting that the Earth Sphere would eventually be dragged into an unprecedented space war, issued a warning: "Humanity no longer has a place to run."

He then established the "Divine Crusaders (DC)," an organization to develop anti-alien combat weapons applying the aliens' Extra-Over Technology (EOT). At the same time, the anti-alien military force "SDF" (Federation Special Space Force), featuring two super-massive space battleships as flagships, was formed. However, these projects required massive costs, reaching the point of worsening the Earth Sphere's economic situation.

Furthermore, many people were not informed of the fact that intelligent extraterrestrial life exists. The space residents (Spacenoids), in particular, rebelled against the Earth Federation Government's tyranny and began launching anti-Earth Federation movements in various regions.

Then, as if responding to the chaos in the Earth Sphere, the Principality of Zeon forces that had fled to the asteroid Axis rose up again as the "Neo Zeon Army" and commenced military operations. In response, the upper echelons of the Earth Federation Forces formed the special forces "Titans" and the special task force "OZ," beginning a hunt for Zeon remnants and the suppression of the Spacenoids' anti-Earth Federation movements.

Meanwhile, on Earth, the Dinosaur Empire and the Demon Empire appeared and attacked the Far East region, but they were annihilated by the actions of "Mazinger Z," "Getter Robo," and "Raideen." Dr. Hell, who had been cooperating with the Dinosaur Empire, and Prince Sharkin of the Demon Empire sought an opportunity to rise again, each reinforcing their respective armies.

And in the year 187 of the New Western Calendar... due to the secret maneuvers of various organizations, the Earth Sphere was once again enveloped in a tense atmosphere...
