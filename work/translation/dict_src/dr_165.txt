#165 Gundam Deathscythe Hell
Duo's wrecked Deathscythe, secretly rebuilt on the Moon by Professor G and the other four Gundam designers.
The main changes are space combat tuning and the Active Cloak, with anti-beam coating. Its beam scythe is now double-bladed for more power.
#166 Gundam Deathscythe Hell Custom
Another form of the Deathscythe Hell. Its enlarged Active Cloak makes it even more menacing, evoking the God of Death. Beside the white-winged Wing Zero Custom, this black-winged unit looks like the very pair of angel and devil (or reaper).
After the battle with the Mariemaia Army, its role done like the other Gundams, Duo blew it up for good, wishing for peace.
#167 Gundam Sandrock
One of the five colony-made Gundams that descended to Earth in Operation Meteor. Built in the L4 colonies by Instructor H. Armored in gundanium alloy, it has great defense and output. Its main weapons are twin heat shotels, able to cut OZ mobile suits in two. Its pilot Quatre loves it dearly, and wept when he blew it up to protect his friends.
OZ repaired the wreck, and Sally Po later reunited Quatre with his Sandrock.
#168 Gundam Sandrock Kai
The ground-type Sandrock, refitted for space by Howard, Duo's support crew.
It also carries a beam machine gun to engage distant foes.
In the second half of the anime, it is fitted with the ZERO System once, when Quatre leads the Gundam Team against a horde of mobile dolls.
#169 Gundam Sandrock Custom
Another form of the Gundam Sandrock.
Its silhouette and colors changed, and its heat shotels grew larger. It has no ranged weapons besides its head vulcans, making it a pure melee unit. It first appeared in option armor and a cloak. It also serves as the commander unit of the Maganac Corps, with strong comms and search.
After the battle with the Mariemaia Army, its role done like the other Gundams, Quatre blew it up for good, wishing for peace.
#170 Gundam Heavyarms
One of the five colony-made Gundams that descended to Earth in Operation Meteor. Built in the L3 colonies by Doktor S.
Made for artillery combat, it is covered in heavy weapons. Once out of ammo, it fights up close with the army knife on its right arm. Trowa is its pilot, but Heero borrowed it for his duel with Zechs in Antarctica.
If hit, its stored ammo should explode, making it very dangerous, but like the other Gundams it is built of tough gundanium alloy, so maybe it is not such a problem.
#171 Gundam Heavyarms Kai
The ground-type Heavyarms, refitted for space by Howard, Duo's support crew. Its left-arm beam gatling is now doubled, greatly raising its firepower.
#172 Gundam Heavyarms Custom
Another form of the Gundam Heavyarms. Its silhouette and colors changed, and against the Mariemaia Army it wielded a double gatling gun in each hand. It wears an optional clown mask, purpose unknown (maybe just for show). It also pulled off stunts, like mimicking Trowa's acrobatics and surviving atmospheric entry fully loaded with explosives.
After the battle with the Mariemaia Army, its role done like the other Gundams, Trowa blew it up for good, wishing for peace.
#173 Shenlong Gundam
One of the five colony-made Gundams that descended to Earth in Operation Meteor. Built in the L5 colonies by Master O. Armored in gundanium alloy.
Second only to the Wing in versatility, it excels at melee at slightly longer range with the extending Dragon Fang on its right arm. Its pilot Wufei calls it "Nataku."
#174 Altron Gundam
Wufei's wrecked Shenlong Gundam, secretly rebuilt on the Moon by Master O and the other four Gundam designers.
Besides improved space combat, the Dragon Fire and Dragon Fang, on one arm in the Shenlong, are now on both. A double beam cannon on its back lets it hit distant foes. "Altron" means "twin-headed dragon." Grabbing foes, breathing fire: it has more attack variety than any unit but the Heavyarms.
#175 Altron Gundam Custom
Another form of the Altron, aka Gundam Nataku (not so named in the anime). It lacks the back beam cannon, but its larger arm Dragon Fangs make it a specialist in short and mid range. Following his own convictions, Wufei turned against the other Gundam pilots in it for a time. In the anime its Dragon Fangs stretch far past spec, but that is just for show, so let it slide.
After the battle with the Mariemaia Army, apart from the other four, Wufei also blew up Nataku for good, its role done.
#176 Gundam Epyon
The Gundam for the vanquished, built at Treize's order. Developed by studying the Tallgeese and the five Gundams, it surpasses them all, and can transform into MA Mode. Like the other Gundams it is armored in gundanium alloy, and like the Wing Zero it has the ZERO System.
Its only weapons are a heat rod and beam saber wired to its generator, with no ranged arms (though the heat rod might count...). So it is less a melee MS than a dueling MS.
Treize gave it to Heero, but he traded units with Milliardo, who had the Wing Zero. "Epyon" is Greek for "next."
#177 Gundam Epyon (MA Mode)
The Gundam Epyon's MA (mobile armor) mode, used mainly for travel.
Its legs swing up from the back to form its nose, making it look less like a fighter than a two-headed dragon. Is it just us, or does it look like the Hambrabi's MA form?
#178 Tallgeese
The first combat mobile suit ever (in the Gundam Wing world), built by Doctor J and others as the model for all later units. Also called the Prototype Leo.
One unit alone can turn the tide of battle, but it demands far more than a normal pilot can give. Above all, the G-forces of its acceleration can crush a pilot to death. It sat in storage with no one to fly it, until Zechs had it restored to fight the Gundams on Earth. Its name seems to mean "necromancer."
#179 Tallgeese II
Treize's Tallgeese, assembled from spare parts for Zechs's unit. Despite the "II," it is basically the same model, with the same performance. Its head looks more like a Gundam's, and its colors differ.
Treize flew it in his duel with Wufei's Altron Gundam.
#180 Tallgeese III
Built alongside Treize's Tallgeese II as the prototype of the Gundam Epyon. Besides a new look it has added weapons: the heat rod also used by the Epyon, and a mega cannon rivaling the Wing Zero's Twin Buster Rifle.
Milliardo, missing since the final battle between OZ and White Fang and known as "Wind" the firefighter, flew it. In its debut it left the atmosphere alone to close in on the resource satellite MO-III seized by the Mariemaia Army, so its amazing thrust seems to have been boosted even more.
#181 Leo
A mass-produced MS of the Alliance and OZ, with many variants and colors for each group. Named after the constellation Leo. Solid enough that Heero's group used it at times.
#182 Aries
A flying MS used by OZ.
It has a simple transformation that folds its legs in flight. Many were mass-produced and deployed to bases everywhere. In the anime Noin flies one in other colors.
Named after the constellation Aries.
#183 Taurus
A mass-produced MS built by OZ.
Built for space, but usable in the atmosphere. It can turn into a flight mode, and outpaces earlier mass-produced units. Standard units are mainly black, while those of Noin's Sanc Kingdom defense are mainly white. Named after the constellation Taurus.
#184 Taurus (Flight Mode)
The Taurus's flight mode.
It can fly in the atmosphere, with much greater mobility.
#185 Taurus (MD)
OZ's transformable space MS Taurus fitted with the Mobile Doll System, an unmanned combat program. Its arms and all else match the manned unit.
#186 Taurus (MD) (Flight Mode)
The flight mode of the mobile doll Taurus. As in MS form, its arms and all else match the manned unit.
#187 Vayeate
An MS OZ had the five Gundam designers build, with the concept of "the strongest unit." Armored in gundanium alloy. It leaves defense to its sister unit Mercurius and is designed to focus purely on long-range fire.
Built for pure firepower, its beam cannon outguns even the Wing Gundam's Buster Rifle. Trowa, who had infiltrated OZ, flew it, and ended up fighting Quatre's Wing Zero. It was later fitted with the Mobile Doll System.
It is named after an ancient Egyptian god.
#188 Mercurius
An MS OZ had the five Gundam designers build, with the concept of "the strongest unit." Armored in gundanium alloy.
It leaves attack to its sister unit Vayeate, and handles defense with Planet Defensors that block beams.
So it carries only minimal arms. Heero, captured by OZ, flew it, and ended up fighting Quatre's Wing Zero. It was later fitted with the Mobile Doll System. Named after Mercury.
#189 Dunbine
A prototype aura battler built by Shot Weapon and the surface engineer Zeit Wright on Drake's orders. Compared with the Drumlo built at the same time, its armor is thin, but it is fast and agile. Its performance directly reflects the pilot's aura power, making it hard to handle, but with strong aura it can match or beat later units. First flown by Sho and later given to Marvel, it fights from the start of the anime to the end. Three were made, including Sho's.
#190 Dunbine (Todd)
An aura battler of the same model as Sho's Dunbine, in other colors. Mainly dark blue, it is flown by Todd Guinness. After Marvel shot it down, it was recovered as spare parts for Sho's unit. Besides Sho's and Todd's, there was also a dark green unit flown by Tokamak Lobsky.
#191 Billbine
The newest transformable aura battler, built by Na. It can turn into a wing caliber, reaching and leaving the field faster than any earlier model. Fast and agile, it also has rapid-fire weapons, the aura cannon and aura sword rifle, excelling at mid range. It also has a new weapon, the aura beam sword. But it needs strong aura power to master, so Queen Ciela Lapana of Na entrusted it to Sho, whom she saw as a true Holy Warrior.
#192 Wing Caliber
The high-speed cruise form of the transformable aura battler Billbine. A "wing caliber" is an aura machine that carries and supports aura battlers. It cannot melee, the aura battler's main style, but it suits long, fast travel, and in the anime it often carried other aura battlers to the front.
#193 Billbine (Night Camo)
The Billbine in night camo for the final battle with Drake. In the anime it performs just like the normal one.
#194 Wing Caliber (Night Camo)
Cruise form of the night-camo Billbine for the final battle with Drake. It performs just like the normal one.
#195 Botune
A mass-produced aura battler built jointly by Rau and Na. Evolved from the Bozune and strong in the air, it was the anti-Drake mainstay. Production units are greenish, but Marvel's is reddish, and King Foizon of Rau's own unit is dark blue.
#196 Gran-Garan
Na's aura battleship, captained by Kawasse Gu. Unlike the others, its vertical build suits 3D combat.
#197 Goraon
The aura battleship of Rau. After King Foizon's death, his granddaughter Ele leads it with captain Abe Tamari. Its bow Aura Nova Cannon gives it great power. The largest of the four aura battleships.
#198 Drumlo
An aura battler built by Shot on Drake's orders. Compared with the Dunbine built at the same time, it has thicker armor and built-in guns. Stable and easy to handle, it was mass-produced as the mainstay of Drake's army until late in the war. Mainly red, but there was also Todd's own unit in dark blue.
#199 Bastole
A prototype aura battler of Drake's army. Its basic design was by the surface engineer Zeit Wright, but Shot's scheming let it draw out a Common's aura power to the limit. As Garalia Nyamhee's personal unit it fully drew out her aura, and in battle with Sho's Dunbine it caused the Aura Road to open. A few were later mass-produced by Zeit and Shot.
