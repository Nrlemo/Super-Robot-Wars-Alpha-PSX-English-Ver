#290 Graydon
The Campbellians' large flying fortress, with eyes on its seven radial bridges. It teleports to attack and retreat.
#291 Voltes V
A combining super robot built with super electromagnetic physics by Dr. Kentaro Go (the Boazanian La Gour), his wife Dr. Mitsuyo Go and Dr. Hamaguchi, foreseeing Boazan's invasion.
It forms from five Volt Machines: Volt Cruiser, Volt Bomber, Volt Panzer, Volt Frigate and Volt Lander. On the call "Let's Volt In!" they fly in a V (V Together) and combine. Main pilot: Kenichi Go. Its finishers include the Tenkuken, whose field severs molecular bonds (the V-Slash, among other cuts). In the anime it gained the Ultra Magcon, the Chodenji Ball and more, and even a warp drive in the finale.
#292 Volt Cruiser
Volt Machine 1, a fighter forming Voltes V's head and computer section. Flown by eldest brother Kenichi, it is the fastest Volt Machine.
#293 Volt Bomber
Volt Machine 2, a heavy bomber forming Voltes V's arms and weapons section. Flown by Ippei, it is as strong as the Volt Cruiser.
#294 Volt Panzer
Volt Machine 3, a heavy tank forming Voltes V's torso and energy section. Driven by Daijiro. Made for land, but unlike the Battle Tank it can fly.
#295 Volt Frigate
Volt Machine 4, forming Voltes V's legs. Piloted by Hiyoshi, youngest of the Go brothers, it also works as a submarine and carries repair gear.
#296 Volt Lander
Volt Machine 5, forming Voltes V's feet. Piloted by Megumi, General Oka's daughter, it mainly scouts and searches. Its nose drill lets it work underground.
#297 Boazan Saucer
A Boazan interstellar ship. It is really bigger than Voltes.
#298 Dokugaga
A beast fighter in episode 1, "Invaders from Space," with huge moth wings. Beast fighters are semi-organic weapons: creatures of the target planet, enhanced and enlarged.
#299 Baizanga
A beast fighter of Boazan's invasion force. It appears in episode 2, "Advance into Hardship," tasked with luring Voltes V away so Big Falcon could be attacked.
#300 Enhanced Namazungo

The anime has a beast fighter named Namazungo, and this is its enhanced version (why "enhanced" is unknown...). To beat it, the new super electromagnetic generator Ultra Magcon was used while still unfinished.
#301 Daimon
An armored beast fighter, enhanced with a full coat of Maxingal alloy. It teamed with its twin, Simon, against Voltes.
#302 Simon
An armored beast fighter clad in Maxingal alloy, seen in episode 26. To exploit a flaw in the Chodenji Ball (no rapid refire), it teamed with its twin Daimon. The alloy came from the Boazan general Do Bergan; at first even the Tenkuken could not cut it.
#303 Skullrook
A battle carrier of Boazan's invasion force. Strong in combat, it can also warp from Boazan to Earth.
#304 Guardian Godol
A combat robot hidden in the statue of Godol, Boazan's guardian god. It appears in the finale, "The Crumbling Tower of Evil!!" It appears when Heinel follows Boazan's legend: "Throw yourself into the blazing fire of Godol, and he shall save the nation." Guided into its cockpit, Heinel fights a last duel with Voltes V at Golden Castle, the palace of Emperor Zu Zambajil.
#305 Dancouga
A super robot formed by the four beast machines of Dr. Kotaro Hazuki, their ultimate form. It debuts in episode 16. Main pilot: Shinobu Fujiwara. Its kanji name, "Dan-Ku-Ga," means "I become emptiness and sever all desire."
It takes superhuman willpower to control, so the team mastered each beast mode step by step, and the combine code stayed sealed until they were ready.
Immensely strong, it first fought almost only with its fists. Later the Dankuu Sword, Daigun and more were added, and a booster let it fly. It was upgraded several times with the dragon mothership Gandole. Its finisher, the Dankuu Kouga Sword, is from the OVA, where the Dankuu Sword draws Gandole's energy.
#306 Eagle Fighter N
An air-combat beast machine Dr. Hazuki built from a Federation fighter, the only one that flies. Like the others, its beast circuit feeds on the pilot's rage and emotions, and it can change modes. Meant for scouting and air support, but like Shinobu, it loves to fight up close.
#307 Eagle Fighter H
The Eagle Fighter's humanoid mode. Handheld weapons allow more flexible tactics. It cannot fly in this mode.
#308 Eagle Fighter A
The Eagle Fighter's aggressive beast mode. Rather than transform, it wraps itself in a pale blue aura (or so) and rams the enemy.
#309 Land Cougar N
A mid-size vehicle-type beast machine built by Dr. Kotaro Hazuki. With the Land Liger it leads the Cyber Beast Force on land, trading some armor for mobility. Like the others it has a beast circuit that feeds on the pilot's rage and other emotions, and can change modes. It forms Dancouga's left foot.
#310 Land Cougar H
The Land Cougar's humanoid mode. Handheld weapons allow more flexible tactics. The most accurate shooter of the beast machines, it serves as a sniper.
#311 Land Cougar A
The Land Cougar's aggressive beast mode. It becomes a black panther, striking with claws and fangs with great agility. Truly beastlike, it shines in close melee against many foes packed together.
#312 Land Liger N
A mid-size tank-type beast machine built by Dr. Hazuki. It leads the land assault with the Land Cougar, the most balanced of the four. Its beast circuit feeds on the pilot's rage and emotions, and it can change modes. It forms Dancouga's right foot.
#313 Land Liger H
The Land Liger's humanoid mode. Handheld weapons allow more flexible tactics.
#314 Land Liger A
The Land Liger's aggressive beast mode. It becomes a male lion, striking with claws and fangs with great agility. Truly beastlike, it shines in close melee against many foes packed together.
#315 Big Moth N
A heavy tank-type beast machine built by Dr. Hazuki, with the most firepower, power and armor of the four, but slow. Its beast circuit feeds on the pilot's rage and emotions, and it can change modes. It backs the Land Cougar and Land Liger up front, and spearheads charges with its bulk. It forms most of Dancouga's body.
#316 Big Moth H
The Big Moth's humanoid mode. Handheld weapons allow more flexible tactics. Its pilot Ryo Shiba is a Chinese kenpo master, so it can also fight hand to hand.
#317 Big Moth A
The Big Moth's aggressive beast mode. It becomes a mammoth, excelling at power attacks: tusk charges, trunk grabs and throws, and stomps using its weight.
#318 Black Wing N
A large transformable fighter flown by Alan, the Black Knight. It has a humanoid mode but no beast mode. It often saved the Cyber Beast Force.
#319 Black Wing H
The Black Wing's humanoid mode. It carries no handheld weapons, fighting mainly with its fists and built-in weapons.
#320 Raideen
A mystic super robot built 12,000 years ago by the science of the ancient Mu Empire. It rests in the God Face Rock as a golden base body, and moves when Akira enters it ("fade in"). It runs on the mysterious energy Mutron, and its Mutron metal armor repairs itself when it returns to its base body. Much of it remains unexplained; late in the anime it showed its true power with the Star of La Mu, a Mutron energy release device. Its main finisher is the God Bird, a charge at Mach 7 in bird form. In the anime it also took God Bird form to cover long distances. Only Akira, of the blood of Mu's King La Mu, can pilot Raideen, controlling it by moving as one with it.
#321 Bluegar
An all-purpose fighter of Mutropolis, used for combat, scouting, surveys and rescue. It has a crew of three, and its cockpit top is the Spit Fighter, a small craft and escape pod.
#322 Drome
A small Demon Empire combat creature with soft, jellyfish-like skin and a will of its own. Swarms led by the Gante act as the fossil beasts' vanguard.
#323 Fossil Beast Bastodon

#324 Gante

#329 Gunbuster
The Earth Empire Space Force's trump card against the Space Monsters: an invincible, ultra-dreadnought-class machine weapon.
It forms by combining Buster Machines 1 and 2. It runs on two degeneracy reactors, one in each, with armor of buster alloy and space titanium. Noriko handles basic movement via the Direct Motion Control System, which passes the pilot's moves straight to the machine. Kazumi handles fire control.
Many of its weapons draw on (?) classic finishers of the past. The Buster Home Run is never used in the anime; it really means batting back an energy ball fired by a Space Monster. The Buster Tomahawk Boomerang is a Super Robot Wars original.
#330 Buster Machine 1
A space warship forming Gunbuster's upper body, with one degeneracy reactor. Even alone it can hold its own against Space Monsters.
#331 Buster Machine 2
A space warship forming Gunbuster's lower body, with one degeneracy reactor. Even alone it can hold its own against Space Monsters.
#332 Sizzler Black
A mass-produced machine weapon based on Gunbuster, read "Sizzler Black." It has one degeneracy reactor to Gunbuster's two, and is a size smaller. Several fight the Space Monsters at the galactic core in episode 6. Sister units Sizzler Silver and White exist; Jung flew the Black after giving Buster Machine 2 to Kazumi.
#333 Exelion
A 4th-generation ultra-dreadnought of the Space Force, built in the Kou Project, flagship of the fleet sent to wipe out the Space Monsters. It has breakthroughs like the Vanishing Motor warp engine. It holds some 25,000 crew, 1,220 machine weapons and 880 interceptors. Its living area is city-sized, with its own railway (express trains included) and two parks, "Nice Beach" and "Mount Koya." Built at a Lagrange point, it could be seen from orbit. Captain: fleet admiral Tatsumi Tashiro.
#334 Space Monster Soldier
Space's immune system: they nest in stars and wipe out a bacteria called intelligent life, the foe of all thinking beings. Soldiers are the smallest type, living on cruiser-class monsters. On finding a foe they detach and rampage on instinct, living only 2 to 3 hours, but fight on par with machine weapons.
