#160 Zechs Merquise | Zechs | Takehito Koyasu
Ace pilot of OZ, the Alliance special forces. Known as the Lightning Baron, and as the Lightning Count after his promotion.
In his first clash with Heero, despite a huge gap in machine performance, he downed the Wing Gundam with a Leo by using free fall. Eager to fight Heero, he had the self-destructed Wing Gundam rebuilt on his own.
But this was treason to OZ, and it led him to leave OZ.
He then took his true name, Milliardo Peacecraft, and acted as one of those who moved the era.
#161 Milliardo Peacecraft | Milliardo | Takehito Koyasu
The true identity of Zechs Merquise.
Relena's brother and prince of the Sanc Kingdom, which once preached total pacifism. After leaving OZ and much wandering, he becomes leader of White Fang, radicals for colony independence, and starts a massive war with Treize's OZ.
But the war was not for gain; it was a rite of passage to show people the folly of war. On the giant battleship Libra as it falls to Earth, with all mankind watching, he fights his last duel with his rival Heero.
#162 Lucrezia Noin | Noin | Chisa Yokoyama
An officer of OZ, the Alliance special forces.
She adores Zechs, so deeply that she knows exactly how long they were apart. She trained OZ pilots at Lake Victoria Base, but lost many cadets near graduation when Wufei bombed their dorm.
After Zechs left OZ, she worked to help his sister Relena revive the Sanc Kingdom.
She joined the Gundam pilots and stood against Zechs when he started a war, but in the end chose to watch over his fight.
#163 Treize Khushrenada | Treize | Ryotaro Okiayu
A Romefeller Foundation leader and commander of OZ, the Alliance special forces. He seeks elegance in all things.
Though young, his absolute charisma has won him many fanatical followers among OZ's young soldiers.
A master politician, he is also superb as an MS pilot and in hand-to-hand combat.
Believing that people are beautiful when they fight, he opposed Romefeller's unmanned Mobile Doll project and was confined for a time. He later became world leader and waged war on Milliardo's White Fang. Like Milliardo, his hidden aim was to make all mankind see the folly of war by waging a great war himself.
Aware of his own sins, he remembers the number and names of all who died in his battles.
He let Wufei strike him down in a dramatic death, which to Wufei was like quitting while ahead.
#164 Lady Une | Une | Yuri Sha
An OZ officer directly under Treize. Rank: special lieutenant. She worships Treize and his ideals, and will stop at nothing to make them real.
Her excess once earned Treize's rebuke: "Do things elegantly, Lady."
Shot by OZ's chief engineer Tsubarov, who saw Treize as an obstacle, she went missing for a time, but later came to Treize's aid.
A harsh past seems to have split her personality, but her two selves finally become one.
#165 Lady Une | Une | Yuri Sha
Lady Une's other personality.
Saint-like, without glasses and in different clothes, she looks like another person. She worked hard in space to carry out OZ's policy of winning over the colonies.
But those around her were quite confused when she sometimes reverted to Lady Une the soldier.
#166 Dorothy Catalonia | Dorothy | Naoko Matsui
Granddaughter of Duke Dermail, head of the Romefeller Foundation; a girl who openly loves war.
Sent to Relena's Peacecraft Academy, she confused Relena with her love of war.
Curious about Heero and the other Gundam pilots, she joined White Fang when they went to space, becoming one of its leaders.
Her love of war is the flip side of her hatred of it, born of her father's death in war.
#167 Duke Dermail | Dermail
Head of the Romefeller Foundation and Dorothy's grandfather. He schemes to rule the Earth Sphere through the Foundation. After crushing the Sanc Kingdom, he made Relena his puppet to grow his power, but her charisma brought about his fall instead.
Stripped of his voice, he headed for space and was killed by White Fang.
#168 Sho Zama | Sho | Shigeru Nakahara
A young Japanese man summoned to Byston Well through the Aura Road opened by the Ferario Silky Mau on Drake's orders.
At first he went with the flow, but meeting Marvel, he chose to turn against Drake. With great aura power, he flies the Dunbine and Billbine at the core of the anti-Drake forces, and his allies rely on him as a Holy Warrior.
Just an ordinary modern youth, he often felt awkward being treated that way, but as the war moved to the surface, he came to accept his role, and served fully as a warrior and as Neal's right hand.
His hobbies are motorcycles and karate. He is very attached to the big bike summoned to Byston Well with him, and worries about it several times early in Dunbine.
To confirm it is really him, ask why a rabbit's eyes are red; he answers, "Because it eats carrots."
#169 Marvel Frozen | Marvel | Mika Doi
An American woman summoned to Byston Well before Sho by the Ferario Knuckle Bee. Her family owns a ranch in Texas. Sharing the anti-Drake cause and drawn to Neal, she aids the Given family.
She showed Sho the evil of Drake and won him over, then returned his feelings, and they raised each other's aura power.
She studied Eastern thought; intelligent, but capable of fierce emotion.
She flew the anti-Drake side's newest aura battlers, the Dana O'Shee, Bozune and Botune, and finally inherited Sho's Dunbine.
She has few notable solo feats, but as Sho's partner and a surface dweller she proved worthy of a Holy Warrior, and is famed among Drake's foes.
#170 Chum Huau | Chum | Maria Kawamura
A Mi Ferario who aids the Given family.
At first she loved Neal and half saw Sho, then on Drake's side, as an enemy, but she became his dear partner.
In battle she shouts orders at Sho, half screaming, and is a big help scouting and confusing foes. She is good at housework, and made her own battle outfit worn later in Dunbine. Cute as a fairy, figures of her were made after she was seen on the surface. Dunbine is said to be the tale Chum, left alone on the surface after the last battle, told the people there.
It was she who named the "Aura Slash" in Dunbine.
#171 El Fino | El | Michie Tomizawa
A Mi Ferario serving Queen Ciela.
She acts as a big sister to Bell, and often teases her.
She is only a comfort to Ciela, and never goes to battle like Chum, but in her first appearance she played a key role, leading Sho to the captive Ciela.
#172 Bell Earl | Bell | Konami Yoshida
A Mi Ferario serving Queen Ciela.
Younger than El or Chum, she looks and acts like a small child.
She asks honest, childlike questions and throws a fit when El mocks her, staying by Ciela as the Gran-Garan's mascot.
#173 Neal Given | Neal | Makoto Ataka
Son of Romun Given, a lord of the Kingdom of Ah. He fought beside his father to stop Drake, but at first his love for Rimul came first, and he often seemed spoiled and unreliable.
But he grew through the war, uniting the anti-Drake forces with his sense of duty, and led the strike team on the front line.
After his ship Zelana sank just before the final battle, he flew a Botune and slew Drake as a pilot.
#174 Keen Kiss | Keen | Yumi Takada
Daughter of the Kiss family, who have long served the Givens.
She felt guilty that her father Kibbutz reluctantly sided with Drake to protect the house. She loves Neal, who loves Rimul, and sometimes acts recklessly to win his approval or out of rivalry with Rimul.
At first she flew the wing caliber Fow, and later an aura battler she got from Marvel.
#175 Ele Hanm | Ele | Run Sasaki
Daughter of King Pinegan of Mi and granddaughter of King Foizon of Rau. After Foizon's death she commands the Goraon as Rau's princess, leading the anti-Drake side. Her unique aura power, called spirit power, lets her foresee and sense evil aura. Yet she is a young girl, who wept over the death of Tolstoy, a surface man she loved, until Marvel and Keen spoke to her. From then on she does her duty as princess and purger of evil aura. In the final battle she risked her life absorbing the Black Knight's evil aura, preventing his Hyper transformation.
#176 Ciela Lapana | Ciela | Miki Takahashi
Queen of Na. Called the Holy Maiden for her noble aura, she always carries a queen's dignity and grace. Out of duty she had the Gran-Garan built and leads the anti-Drake side.
She saw Sho, who saved her, as a true Holy Warrior, and entrusted him with the new aura battler Billbine. Grieving that aura machines were changing Byston Well, herself included, she resolved to scrap them all once Drake fell. On the surface she fought Drake while trying to keep the surface from chaos, even meeting the Queen of England. In the final battle in the Pacific, she released all her aura power, purified all evil aura and ended the war.
#177 Abe Tamari | Abe | Ryuichi Horibe
A retainer of Rau.
As captain of the aura ship Grimrey, he supplied Neal's group with Botunes and more, and delivered the Billbine that Queen Ciela of Na entrusted to Sho.
After King Foizon's death he captained the Goraon, serving as Ele's right hand.
He once punched a hotheaded Sho to set him straight.
In the final battle, holding Ele, who stopped the Black Knight's Hyper transformation at the cost of her life, he rams the Gyre-Garing with the Goraon, his heart set on Byston Well.
#178 Kawasse Gu | Kawasse | Shingo Hiromori
A retainer of Na.
As captain of the Gran-Garan, he directs the battle in practice.
A loyal servant who worries for Ciela even in combat.
#179 Rimul Luft | Rimul | Kyoko Irokawa
Drake's only daughter.
She loves Neal of the enemy Given family, and fearing her father's ambitions, secretly leaked Drake's secrets.
She ran away many times, and once Neal even rescued her and she traveled with him. Raised as a lady but feisty, she sometimes flew aura battlers recklessly to win his approval.
But she was brought back, and later in Dunbine she was on Bishot's Gyre-Garing with her mother Luxet.
She told Drake of Luxet's betrayal, and tried to slay her mother, the root of this war.
#180 Todd Guinness | Todd | Hidemi Aisaka
An American summoned to Drake's side with Sho. Pragmatic and selfish, he sides with Drake, who promised him a knight's status, even after Rimul tells him of Drake's plots.
Losing to Sho, now his enemy, again and again, his thirst for revenge swells his aura power. After going missing, he returns as a knight of Ku, and his obsession troubles Sho's group. Yet his rivalry with Allen, love for his mother and fixation on Sho reveal a naive heart.
He flew a custom-color Dunbine and a Drumlo, and later a Vierres and Wryneck. Just before the final battle, his hate for Sho makes his aura explode, turning the Wryneck Hyper.
#181 Garalia Nyamhee | Garalia | Shinobu Adachi
Born to a knightly house of Ah, she holds high rank among Drake's knights despite being a woman. Raised as the daughter of a knight who deserted, she has extreme ambition and fighting spirit, and uses any means to reach her goals.
She flies a Bastole that she egged on the surface engineer Zeit Wright to tune for her. In battle her amplified aura power carried her to the surface with Sho. After much there, she grew close to Sho, and they tried to return to Byston Well together.
But her aura was drained on the surface, her body could not survive the Aura Road, and she never returned to Byston Well.
#182 Burn Bunnings | Burn | Sho Hayami
Captain of Drake's knights. A top aura battler pilot, he also commanded the summoned Sho and the others.
At first Drake trusted him, but repeated failures cost him his post. Humiliated, he throws away pride and past glory to hunt Sho as his nemesis. Yet he loses every time, until he casts off his very name and face to live as "the Black Knight."
His aura, swollen with rage and hate, troubles even Sho and his Billbine.
On the surface he keeps growing stronger out of revenge, until he turns Hyper in the final battle. Elle's sacrifice stops it, but he makes a last stand against Sho with aura swollen by grudges.
#183 Drake Luft | Drake | Shoji Oki
A lord of the Kingdom of Ah.
With the surface man Shot Weapon he perfected aura machines, summoning surface dwellers one after another to conquer Byston Well. Shrewd and decisive, with great aura power, his iron-willed ambition rejects even his daughter Rimul's pleas. He overthrew his lord, King Fraon, took the throne of Ah, and built the giant battleship Will-Wips, but just as his conquest began, he was sent to the surface with all the aura machines.
Learning that aura machines grow stronger there, he spread his ambition and war to the surface.
Amid strife in his camp, such as the plots of Bishot and Shot and his wife Luxet's infidelity, he fought Ciela and Elle, and was slain by Neal while leaving the final battle in the Pacific.
#184 Bishot Hatta | Bishot | Kazuyuki Sogabe
King of Ku.
Quick to see the value of Drake's aura machines, he allied with him, and with his own technology built aura battlers like the Vierres and the giant battleship Gyre-Garing.
He works with Drake to conquer Byston Well, but on the surface he has an affair with Drake's wife Luxet, and they plot to kill Drake.
But he is nervous and timid, and Luxet holds all the power.
In the final battle in the Pacific, he sinks with the Gyre-Garing when the Goraon rams it.
#185 Jeril Coochibi | Jeril | Tomoko Otsuka
One of the surface dwellers Drake summoned after Sho. An Irish rock singer with streaked red hair.
Aggressive, she enjoyed the war in Byston Well like a game.
Back on the surface she went from Ireland to Greece, where her aura power put the Greek army under her. Leading air force fighters in her Leprechaun, the pilots called her "the Joan of Arc of the 20th century." But fighting Sho and Marvel, her rage and hate made her aura run wild, causing the first Hyper transformation ever. She cornered the Billbine with that power, but finally destroyed herself, unable to control her aura.
#186 Allen Brady | Allen | Norio Wakamoto
One of the surface dwellers Drake summoned after Sho. A US Air Force ace and Todd's senior.
Cool on the surface, he reads situations well, and knew full well that the summoned surface dwellers were mere mercenaries. He was also crafty enough to use Drake's army himself.
His skill troubled Sho's group, but he lost to Sho, now in the Billbine, in the battle for Rau.
#187 Fei Chenka | Fei | Koichi Hashimoto
One of the surface dwellers Drake summoned after Sho. A Chinese man, a self-styled "struggling actor."
Pushy and glory-hungry, he attacks Sho's group at Musy's invitation.
Sho warns him of the danger of helping Drake, but he will not listen, attacks, and is killed.
#188 Musy Poe | Musy | Mari Yokoo
Drake's retainer, formerly Rimul's music teacher. In love with the surface man Shot Weapon, she fights for him as a warrior along with her father and her two brothers, Orl and Bibi.
Her love for Shot boosts her aura, and with strong aura machines like the Zwarth and Bubury she troubles Sho's group.
As Shot's trusted agent, she even tried to assassinate Drake for his ambitions.
In the final battle she dies with Shot under Sho's and Marvel's attack.
#189 Shot Weapon | Shot | Masahiko Tanaka
A surface engineer summoned to Byston Well. Under Drake, with the surface man Zeit Wright, he developed aura machines, fusing robotics with aura power.
He also joins Drake's bid to conquer Byston Well. But he secretly plans to use Drake to become king himself, and on the surface he acts apart from Drake with his own aura cruiser Spriggan. A schemer even more than an engineer, he advances his plans while dodging Drake's suspicion. In the final battle he has his aide and lover Musy try to assassinate Drake, but she fails. He dies with Musy under Sho's and Marvel's attack.
#190 Garamity Mangan | Garamity | Masaharu Sato
Leader of the "Three Red Knights of Ku."
In a custom-colored Vierres, he uses the three-unit Tripler attack with his comrades Da and Nyet, once knocking Sho out in the skies over the Himalayas.
But when Sho came to, he beat the other two, and Garamity vowed revenge, only to be killed by Sho and Marvel in India.
#191 Da | Da | Issei Futamata
One of the "Three Red Knights of Ku."
In a custom-colored Vierres, he once had Sho in a tight spot with Garamity and Nyet using the Tripler attack.
