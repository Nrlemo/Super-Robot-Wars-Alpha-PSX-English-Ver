#192 Nyet | Nyet | Koji Totani
One of the "Three Red Knights of Ku."
In a custom-colored Vierres, he once knocked Sho out with Garamity and Da using the Tripler attack.
#193 Banjo Haran | Banjo | Hirotaka Suzuoki
Son of Sozo Haran, inventor of the Meganoids and Megaborgs.
He hates his father, who sacrificed his brother and mother to experiments, and the Meganoids that came of it. Years before the story, he stole the Daitarn 3, a Meganoid prototype still in development, and fled Mars.
With the huge store of gold he took, he built a mansion outside Sin the City.
With allies like Beauty and Reika, he fights the Meganoids in the Daitarn 3. He handles all sorts of machines, and his superhuman strength and marksmanship make him formidable on foot too.
A vain, cocky dandy sure of his charm with beautiful women, yet always humorous and often a clown, a truly charming man.
He can be ruthless enough to abandon even friends to defeat the Meganoids, but those around him love and trust him all the same.
The Haran Zaibatsu is an original setting of this game.
#194 Garrison Tokita | Garrison | Koichi Kitamura
Banjo's butler, a man whose origins and past are a total mystery. A brilliant, ever-calm gentleman with keen tactical judgment.
A first-class butler, he does it all, from caring for Banjo's group to gathering intel and maintaining machines, and has even fought in the Daitarn 3 himself. A gourmet who cooks like a top chef.
He demands perfection in his work, and once refused to launch the Daitarn 3, with Banjo in danger, because it had not been waxed. His type is a curvy beauty in her prime, sexy and poised.
He also enjoys watching soap operas.
#195 Reika Sanjo | Reika | Yo Inoue
A former Interpol cadet.
She met Banjo while investigating a Meganoid and has been his assistant ever since. Her Interpol training makes her excellent at shooting, fighting and piloting.
She sometimes clashes with Beauty, his other assistant, but they make a good team. She seems a calm, ladylike beauty, but she is tough, and shows a comic side in her spats with Beauty.
#196 Beautiful Tachibana | Beauty
Banjo's assistant.
A blonde beauty, daughter of a tour company president and a former Miss International.
A spoiled rich girl, innocent and still childish.
But she fights well when pushed. Her feelings for Banjo are strong, and she and Reika are always fighting over him.
Being about as mature as Toppo, she makes a good sparring partner for him too.
#197 Totta Toda | Toppo | Fuyumi Shiraishi
A boy who lost his parents to the Meganoids.
He fought alongside Banjo's group when they met, then moved into Banjo's mansion.
A self-proclaimed "First Assistant" and a bit of a showoff, "hot-blooded, a fireball" with "big dreams." Full of drive, he once took the Daitarn and piloted it on his own.
#198 Koji Kabuto | Koji | Hiroya Ishimaru
Pilot of Mazinger Z.
Grandson of Juzo Kabuto, creator of Mazinger Z, and son of Kenzo, creator of Great Mazinger. Heir to the will of his grandfather, killed by Baron Ashura, he fought Dr. Hell's bid for world conquest. He then moved to the Photon Power Laboratory with his brother Shiro, entering Tojo Academy High. Hot-blooded but easily carried away, he often rushed into traps. A soft-hearted hero trusted by all, a great athlete and an expert biker.
After beating Dr. Hell he studied at NASA with Sayaka, but returned to fight the Mycenae Empire, joining Great Mazinger in Mazinger Z once more.
#199 Sayaka Yumi | Sayaka | Minori Matsushima
Professor Yumi's daughter and pilot of the Aphrodite A and Diana A. She skips high school to help her father at the Photon Power Laboratory. Feisty, she squabbles with Koji but secretly worries for him. So she gets jealous when he falls for pretty girls, which has hurt their teamwork in battle.
After Dr. Hell's defeat she studied at NASA with Koji, but returned to fight again against the Mycenae Empire.
#200 Boss | Boss | Hiroshi Otake
Koji's classmate. Sweet on Sayaka, he sees Koji as a rival. To rival Mazinger Z, he had the Lab's three doctors build him the Boss Borot, and fights with his lackeys Nuke and Mucha, often getting in the way. But he is a big-hearted champion of justice and Koji's irreplaceable friend. After Dr. Hell fell and the Mycenae Empire invaded, he helped the Fortress of Science Lab fight beside Great Mazinger. Like Nuke and Mucha, his real name is unknown (though some say it is Susumu Bota...).
#201 Shiro Kabuto | Shiro
Koji's little brother, a 5th grader at Tojo Academy. Koji is his only family, but he is always cheerful and a support to his brother, even giving him advice at times. After Koji left for NASA, he lived at the Fortress of Science Lab with his father Kenzo, who survived as a cyborg. In Great Mazinger he even fought in his own robot, the Robot Junior.
#202 Professor Yumi | Yumi | Joji Yanami
Real name: Gennosuke Yumi.
Juzo Kabuto's top disciple and director of the Photon Power Laboratory. He built the Aphrodite A and Diana A piloted by his daughter Sayaka, and upgraded Mazinger Z many times in the war with Dr. Hell. Calm and gentle, with a director's sense of duty and judgment. After Dr. Hell's defeat, he left the fight with Mycenae to the Fortress of Science Lab and returned to Photon Power research.
#203 Dr. Nossori | Nossori
A disciple of Dr. Juzo Kabuto and a key Photon Power Laboratory staffer. A leading systems engineer, slow at work but known for precision. He handles Mazinger Z's upgrades. As his name suggests, he is easygoing.
#204 Dr. Morimori | Morimori
A disciple of Dr. Juzo Kabuto and a key Photon Power Laboratory staffer. A leading energy engineer who developed the Iron Cutter and more. As his name suggests, he is energetic. He was killed in an Iron Mask Corps attack during the war with Dr. Hell.
#205 Dr. Sewashi | Sewashi
A disciple of Dr. Juzo Kabuto and key Lab staffer. A top mechanical engineer who repairs Mazinger Z and builds new weapons. As his name suggests, he is impatient.
#206 Photon Lab Staff | Staff
Staff of the Photon Power Laboratory. Besides research, they run radar, barrier and defense systems when the enemy attacks.
#207 Dr. Hell | Hell | Kosei Tomita
An evil genius bent on world conquest. A German expert in atomic physics, engineering, cybernetics and archaeology, and a college classmate of Dr. Juzo Kabuto, creator of Mazinger Z.
He built weapons for the military, then joined a dig on Bardos Island. There he found giant robots left by Mycenae, made them into Mechanical Beasts and set out to rule the world. Ruthless, he uses hostages, assassins and more. After his defeat, the Emperor of Darkness revived him as Great Marshal of Hell, leading the Battle Beasts after the Great General's death.
#208 Baron Ashura | Ashura | Kitahama\nShibata
A cyborg Dr. Hell made from the mummies of a noble couple found on Bardos Island. Utterly loyal, he leads the Iron Mask Corps and Mechanical Beasts from the sea fortresses Salude and Bood. His staff controls the Beasts and fires rays, and he can transform. A cold but sloppy schemer, he loses to Mazinger Z again and again. Yet Hell trusted him, and even wept at his death.
#209 Count Brocken | Brocken | Junpei Takiguchi
A former German officer made a cyborg by Dr. Hell. His head, blown off by a bomb in the war, floats apart from his body via anti-gravity. He was sent to the front as a strategist to end the string of defeats by Mazinger Z. He leads the Iron Cross Corps and Mechanical Beasts from the flying fortress Ghoul. Cruel and proud, he feuds with Baron Ashura for credit. He followed Dr. Hell to the final battle on Hell Island and shared his fate.
#210 Archduke Gorgon | Gorgon | Osamu Kato
A mysterious figure who supplies Dr. Hell with Demon Mechanical Beasts. He is really a Mycenae spy scouting the surface for invasion. His lower half is a giant flying tiger. After Hell's fall, he urged the Great General of Darkness to invade, and spied on Tetsuya's team.
#211 Tetsuya Tsurugi | Tetsuya | Keiichi Noda
Pilot of Great Mazinger.
Taken from an orphanage by Kenzo Kabuto at age 7 to pilot the combat robot Great Mazinger, he underwent brutal training. Raised as a combat professional, he is formidable on foot as well. His past and cynical way of talking make him seem cold and distant, but he is kind and stern, a true champion of justice. He secretly loves Kenzo as a father, but has a deep complex about being an orphan. So he envies Koji, back to fight Mycenae, and Kenzo together, and makes a fatal mistake in the final battle. He was 22 when Great Mazinger began airing, but was later changed to 18 to seem more youthful. In Gosaku Ota's manga, he is a comical yet even tougher character than on TV.
#212 Jun Hono | Jun | Yumi Nakatani
Pilot of the Venus A. Like Tetsuya, she was taken from an orphanage and harshly trained as a combat professional. Usually tough and feisty, she is insecure about her origins and sometimes broods alone.
#213 Kenzo Kabuto | Kenzo
Creator of Great Mazinger and father of Koji and Shiro Kabuto. He was building Mazinger Z with Koji's grandfather Juzo when an accident gravely injured him, and Juzo turned him into a cyborg. He then built Great Mazinger and, as director of the Fortress of Science Lab, fights the Mycenae invasion. He raised the orphans Tetsuya and Jun and loves them as family, but his duty to protect peace forces him to hide it and be ruthless at times. He is ashamed of being a cyborg, and feels guilty he cannot be a real father to Koji and Shiro. In the final battle with Mycenae, Tetsuya refuses to work with Koji out of his orphan complex and falls into danger; Kenzo sacrifices himself to save him.
In the movie "Mazinger Z vs. the Great General of Darkness," he dresses as a prophet (?) and foretells Mycenae's revival and the Boss Borot flying.
#214 Great General of Darkness | Great General | Kenichi Ogata
A general of an enemy of Mycenae, revived as a humanoid Battle Beast after losing to the Emperor of Darkness, Mycenae's ruler. Now the Emperor's loyal servant, he led seven Battle Beast legions to conquer the surface. A brave warrior, he fell in a duel with Great Mazinger. His grieving men showed how they trusted him, proof of a true general. Although the movie "Mazinger Z vs. the Great General of Darkness" hints at a duel with Mazinger Z, he only makes a cameo (which actually showed how mighty he was).
#215 Dante | Dante | Keiichi Noda
A demon-type Mycenae Battle Beast. In the movie "Mazinger Z vs. the Great General of Darkness," he leads the Mycenae vanguard and nearly destroys the Photon Power Laboratory. Koji was shocked by "a talking Mechanical Beast"; viewers were too, on learning he shares TV Tetsuya's voice.
#216 Ryoma Nagare | Ryoma | Akira Kamiya
Pilot of the Eagle/Getter-1 and Dragon/Getter Dragon. Captain of the Asama Academy soccer team, he pilots the Getter Robo in place of Tatsuto, Dr. Saotome's son, killed in the Dinosaur Empire's invasion.
Responsible, earnest and single-minded, a bit inflexible, he leads the Getter Team. His family runs a kendo dojo in Kyushu, and he uses his swordsmanship in battle. He rides a sidecar bike, often with Musashi beside him.
#217 Hayato Jin | Hayato | Keaton Yamada
Pilot of the Jaguar/Getter-2 and Liger/Getter Liger. He refused his classmate Ryoma's plea to pilot the Getter, but volunteered when Michiru, who recalls his late mother, was in danger. A cool, athletic nihilist, his reserve is often misunderstood, and he clashed with the rash Ryoma and Musashi. But he burns quietly with duty, and deep down shares a strong bond with his friends. He plays the harmonica.
#218 Musashi Tomoe | Musashi | Toku Nishio
Pilot of the Bear/Getter-3. Captain of the Asama Academy judo team and an old friend of Ryoma. He pilots the Getter for Ryoma, though his fear of reptiles troubled him at first. Bold and manly, he trained hard to overcome his flaws. Weak for pretty girls, he is crazy about Michiru.
He uses his judo in the cockpit, and loves to upgrade his Getter-3, even fitting the flightless unit with a parachute.
In the last battle with the Dinosaur Empire, blaming himself for the Getter's destruction, he gave his life to destroy the invincible battleship Dai and the Empire.
#219 Benkei Kuruma | Benkei | Joji Yanami
Pilot of the Poseidon and Getter Poseidon. A transfer student at Asama Academy, he met Dr. Saotome's son Genki and was scouted to pilot the Poseidon for his great stamina and strength. He first refused, but when Genki was hurt in a Hyakki Empire attack, he regretted it and joined the fight in the Poseidon.
By nature easygoing and peaceful, he loves animals and children. He loves baseball, and later captains the Asama Academy team. In the original, he never uses Musashi's signature Daisetsuzan Oroshi.
#220 Jack King | Jack | Makio Inoue
Main pilot of the Texas Mack, built in America, and son of its creator, Dr. King. Proud and cocky, he mocked the Getter Team as "three halves of a pilot." His confidence is backed by skill; he fought the Getter Robo to a draw or better in a mock battle. But Getter-3 saved him from a Mechasaurus, and they came to respect each other.
#221 Mary King | Mary | Yumi Nakatani
Jack's sister, who pilots the hat-shaped support and weapons unit of the Texas Mack. Polite, she smooths over Jack's aggressive words, and gives him sharp support in battle.
#222 Dr. Saotome | Saotome | Kosei Tomita
Director of the Saotome Research Institute. He lost his eldest son Tatsuto in the Dinosaur Empire's first attack, leaving a family of four: his wife Kazuko, daughter Michiru and younger son Genki.
A leading Getter Ray researcher, he built the Getter Robo for space, armed it against the Dinosaur Empire, and later built a new combat model. Scruffy in stubble and geta, he is a top scientist who also leads the Getter Team calmly and clearly.
Usually mild, he will even shout down the three when they get too hotheaded.
#223 Michiru Saotome | Michiru
Dr. Saotome's daughter, an Asama Academy student like Ryoma. She helps her father's research and supports the Getter Team by scouting, searching and supplying in her command machine, the Lady Command.
The team's darling, feisty yet gentle, though jealous of pretty guest characters. In the final battle with the Hyakki Empire she flew the Liger in Hayato's place, having seemingly accepted his feelings.
#224 Hyoma Aoi | Hyoma | Yuji Mitsuya
Leader of the Combattler Team and pilot of Battle Jet, Unit 1.
Orphaned in an accident, he was taken in by the Sun Academy orphanage.
A skilled rider, quick to fight and fiery, he is a born genius in battle. Shot through the arm in a fight on foot with Garuda, he was fitted with a high-performance prosthetic.
Cheerful and simple, he is the model of "crush the strong, help the weak," with a fighting spirit that never gives up.
But he is hopelessly shy with women, and slow to respond to Chizuru's advances.
