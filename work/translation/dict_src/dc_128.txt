#128 Karlmann Dukartuse | Karlmann
Orphaned by a BESPA attack, he is rescued by Uso and Shakti. Traveling with the League Militaire, he showed signs of Newtype potential, sensing Fuala's Zanneck attacks and where Shakti was. After the war he lives in Kasarelia with Uso and the others.
#129 Oi Nyung | Nyung
Leader of the League Militaire in Eastern Europe. Said to come from Ameria in Side 2, birthplace of Zanscare, his comrades call him "the Count." Abducted by Chronicle, he is guillotined by Fuala as a warning to anti-BESPA forces.
#130 Romero Marabal | Romero
The League Militaire's chief mechanic. With Uso since Uhig, in the final battle he rams the foe in the Reinforce Jr. with the old men, leaving the future to the young. He seems to be ex-SNRI, maker of the F91.
#131 Otis Arkins | Otis
One of the League Militaire mechanics assisting Romero. He also played the priest at Oliver and Marbet's wedding. With Uso since Uhig, in the final battle he rammed the Zugan fleet in the Reinforce Jr. with the other old men.
#132 Hangelg Ewin | Hangelg
Uso's father, and one of the "Jin Jahanam," the name shared by the League Militaire's leaders. He works hard against Zanscare, winning the Federation's help, but to his son Uso he seems a selfish father who ignores him. In the final battle he was aboard the League's flagship Jeanne d'Arc, and his fate is unknown.
#133 Muller Miguel | Muller
Uso's mother. A League Militaire spy on the Moon, she helped build the Victory Gundam. Captured by BESPA while saving Shakti, she is made a hostage in Operation Earth Clean and dies before Uso's eyes.
#134 Katejina Loos | Katejina | Kumiko Watanabe
A girl from Uhig whom Uso knew through online chats and admired. Burned out of her home, she joined the League Militaire, but went over to BESPA. She became a pilot for Chronicle and fought Uso many times. At first she shared Zanscare's ideals, but her mind grew unstable as the war went on, and she fought Uso with a mix of hatred and love. Shot down by Uso in the final battle, she lost her memory and sight and wandered aimlessly...
She seems to have had strong Newtype powers; in the novels they were boosted by enhancement surgery.
#135 Chronicle Asher | Chronicle | Tomoyuki Dan
A BESPA soldier and Queen Maria's younger brother. Hating to be an elite in name only, he seeks glory at the front.
Testing the prototype MS Shokew, he met Uso by chance and lost the machine to him. He fought Uso, now a League Militaire pilot, many times, obsessed with beating the Victory Gundam. An idealist at times, he clashes with Zanscare's leader Kagatie and with Tassilo, who covets that seat. In the battle at the descending Angel Halo he lost to Uso, dying as he called his sister's name. Hating dust on Earth, he wears a dust mask there.
#136 Fuala Griffon | Fuala | Ai Orikasa
A BESPA officer commanding Rogue Base. Rank: commander. Born to a line of executioners, she sees it as her fate and shows her enemies no mercy.
To crush the anti-BESPA spirit sparked by Uso and the Shrike Team, she guillotines the abducted Nyung on her own. Her enraged superior Tassilo punishes her by setting her adrift in space.
#137 Fuala Griffon | Fuala | Ai Orikasa
Fuala after returning from her exile in space, so terrified she is extremely unstable.
Wearing bells all over to mark her line of executioners, she flies the Zanneck and Gengaozo as Tassilo's own pilot, tormenting the League Militaire. She controlled a psycommu-like device in her machine, so she was likely enhanced.
#138 Metchet Rubence | Metchet | Toshiyuki Morikawa
A BESPA pilot at Rogue Base who flies the Ricarl that carries Fuala into battle.
#139 Arbeo Pippiniden | Pippiniden | Junji Kitajima
A BESPA officer who also fights at the front as a pilot. Rank: captain. Chronicle's senior at the academy, he hopes to stay friends after they meet again on Earth. Highly ambitious, he will do anything to win. He later assists Chronicle in Operation Earth Clean, but Chronicle turns on him for taking Uso's mother Muller hostage. Just before sortieing in the mobile armor Birken-Au, he dies in the blast of Lupe's damaged machine as it lands.
#140 Lupe Cineau | Lupe | Miki Ito
A BESPA pilot who taught Katejina to fly, rising from ensign to captain. Selfish, she climbs by using others and discards them when done, finally latching onto Pippiniden. Yet deep down she rejects her own ways, and sees her ideals in Uso's purity. She once "tortured" him in a bath (?).
#141 Tassilo Vago | Tassilo | Hidetoshi Nakamura
A Zanscare colonel. He will try anything for his ambition, like exiling his own subordinate Fuala, then recovering her himself to serve him. Blamed for losing Kaiilas Guilly, he nearly faces the guillotine, but Kagatie saves him and puts him in charge of defending the Angel Halo. To seize Zanscare, he turns on Kagatie and abducts Queen Maria, but panicking as the battle turns, he shoots her in a rage. Uso, who saw it, burns him with the V2's beam saber.
#142 Duker Iq | Iq | Kazuhiro Nakata
A BESPA officer with a biker's soul, who dreams of making Earth a biker's paradise. He leads the Gattard battle bike unit against Uso's group on Earth. He also devised the bike battleship Adrastea, and rides it in Operation Earth Clean. He falls in love with his aide Renda, and they promise a future after the war, but he dies fighting Uso and Odelo.
#143 Renda de Paroma | Renda | Rica Matsumoto
A BESPA pilot and Duker Iq's aide. Her superior Iq confesses his love, but she dies in the very next battle.
#144 Goze Barl | Goze | Yukitoshi Hori
A BESPA pilot. On Pippiniden's orders he takes Uso's mother Muller hostage in Operation Earth Clean, but Chronicle, who hates cowardice, sends Katejina after him. Attacked by her and Uso, he dies with Muller when their ships collide.
#145 Broho | Broho | Jurota Kosugi
A BESPA pilot of the giant mobile armor Doggorla. He has great faith in its power, and hates seeing women and children on the battlefield.
#146 Godwald Hein | Godwald | Joji Nakata
A BESPA pilot whose skill and fighting spirit live up to his name, "the Man-Eating Tiger." Uso saved him and his men adrift in space. Later, in the prototype MS Abigor, he fights Uso and teaches him a warrior's mindset, but dies when the Abigor explodes.
#147 Maria Pure Armonia | Maria
Queen of Zanscare. Her supernatural powers made her charismatic from a young age. She later preached Mariaism, which centers on comfort and salvation and restoring the mother's place. Kagatie used this creed to found Zanscare in Ameria, a colony in Side 2.
Maria wants peace, but sees some truth in Kagatie's war on the Federation and half tolerates it. She reunites with her daughter Shakti, but is caught up in Tassilo's ambitions, and he shoots her dead before Uso's eyes.
#148 Fonse Kagatie | Kagatie
The true founder of Zanscare and head of the Gachi Party. On his voyage back from Jupiter he decided humanity must change, founded Zanscare, and recruited Maria, a commoner, as its queen and symbol. Ready to do anything for his ideals, he forced through the guillotine and other harsh policies. After Tassilo took Maria from him, he used Shakti to activate the Angel Halo in the final operation, but died as it collapsed.
#149 Heero Yuy | Heero | Hikaru Midorikawa
A young colony agent sent to Earth from the L1 colonies to defeat OZ, the Alliance special forces.
Trained as a warrior from childhood, he is said to be twice as strong as a normal man, an astonishing boy already a perfect saboteur at his age. His power stunned even his fellow agent Duo; he can survive a fall from a 50-story building.
Yet Doctor J, who raised him, says he is gentle at heart, which is why he could not kill Relena, who knew his secret.
In his first battle Zechs knocked his Wing Gundam into the sea. OZ tricked him into shooting down a shuttle of Alliance leaders seeking peace with the colonies, and facing Lady Une's threat of missile strikes on the colonies, he chose to self-destruct mid-battle with Zechs. He fails often as an agent.
But his way of life touched many in those turbulent times; he acted as one of the hidden hands of change, and greeted the new era as "the final victor."
Duo calls him "quiet, rude and reckless."
#150 Duo Maxwell | Duo | Toshihiko Seki
An agent from the L2 colonies.
Taken in by a church amid war as a child, he received the name Maxwell there, and calls himself Duo Maxwell.
His priest-like outfit dates from then.
Naturally cheerful and friendly.
His motto: "I run and I hide, but I never lie." His trademark is a braid down to his thighs. He calls himself the God of Death because "I've never seen a miracle, but I've seen lots of dead people."
Superb in battle, he beat the Vayeate and Mercurius, made into mobile dolls from Heero's and Trowa's data. He takes on many missions, but often draws the short straw, like Heero stealing his Gundam's parts or Trowa destroying his Gundam.
#151 Trowa Barton | Trowa | Shigeru Nakahara
An agent from the L3 colonies.
His name is not his own; circumstances led him to take another man's name.
Calm and rarely emotional, he is loyal to his comrades.
When Quatre went berserk in the Wing Gundam Zero, Trowa risked his life to talk him down; Quatre came to, but Trowa was hit, going missing and losing his memory. Once found, he overcame the ZERO System, which restored his memory. He is also a fine fighter on foot, so agile he can do a triple-twist layout somersault with ease. Using that agility, he hid in a circus on Earth.
He has also shown his talent by playing the flute to Quatre's violin.
#152 Quatre Raberba Winner | Quatre | Ai Orikasa
Only son of the prestigious Winner family of the L4 colonies. His mild manner makes him seem far from conflict, but he is a born genius at piloting mobile suits.
Despite his birth, he left home after a feud with his father, all but disowned.
When selfish colonists killed his father and family, he fell into madness. He built the forbidden Wing Gundam Zero and destroyed colony after colony, until Trowa's selfless plea brought him back.
Near the end he led the five Gundam pilots fighting together, holding the other four together.
He has 29 older sisters, all test-tube babies; only Quatre was born from a womb.
#153 Chang Wufei | Wufei | Ryuzo Ishino
A warrior from the L5 colonies.
He always acts to uphold his own justice, and removes whoever stands in his way without question.
Direct heir of an ancient Chinese warrior clan, he takes pride in his martial arts, and hunts Treize, who beat and humiliated him in single combat, as his nemesis.
He calls his Shenlong Gundam "Nataku," after an ancient Chinese warrior.
Emotional and seemingly rough, he has a keen tactical eye; of the Gundam boys only he saw the true aim of Operation Daybreak.
#154 Doctor J | Doctor J
The man who built the Wing Gundam and raised Heero as an agent.
He was once in OZ with the other four doctors, creating the Tallgeese.
He looks like a mad scientist, but beyond his mission he is very human, telling Relena that Heero is really a gentle boy.
#155 Relena Darlian | Relena
Princess of the Peacecraft royal family of the Sanc Kingdom, a small Nordic nation that preached total pacifism. After the kingdom fell, she was raised as the daughter of Vice Foreign Minister Darlian, a former elder.
Meeting the Gundam pilot Heero and Darlian's assassination lead her to learn her origins, and her fate changes.
She quietly revives the Sanc Kingdom in a corner of Europe, but the Romefeller Foundation sees her ideals as a threat, and the kingdom falls again.
Through many hardships, her sincerity, drive and charisma make her one of those who move history.
After the war, she becomes Vice Foreign Minister, a bridge between the colonies and Earth.
#156 Catherine Bloom | Catherine
Star performer of the circus where Trowa hid. Her specialty is knife throwing, and so many fans come to see her that the ringmaster cannot say no to her.
When Trowa wandered with amnesia, she took him in as her brother.
Strong enough to punch Trowa when he tried to self-destruct, and kind enough to try to stop the other Gundam pilots from taking him back to war.
#157 Sally Po | Sally
An Alliance army doctor who later joined the anti-OZ guerrillas.
Often near the Gundam pilots, she worked to support them. When Wufei lost heart after losing to Treize, she spurred him on and got him back into the Shenlong.
#158 Hilde Schbeiker | Hilde
An OZ volunteer.
Fooled by OZ's policy of winning over the colonies, she learned the truth from Duo and joined him.
Near the end, she infiltrated the giant battleship Libra alone. Gravely wounded, she got its inside data to the Gundam pilots.
#159 Pargan | Pargan
Relena's butler, once a servant of the Peacecraft family.
Behind his mild looks is a very capable man, able to track down Heero and the other Gundam pilots.
He is also said to be a master fencer, who once taught young Milliardo.
