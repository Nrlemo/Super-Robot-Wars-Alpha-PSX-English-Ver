#62 Marasai
A mass-produced MS built by Anaheim Electronics and used by the Titans. A stronger generator lets it use two or more beam weapons at once, which the previous mainstay, the Hizack, could not.
#63 Messala
The MS mode of a transformable mobile armor built by Scirocco. It can use beam sabers and more for close combat. In the anime it was the first transformable unit seen, and its transformation stunned Kamille and Emma.
#64 Messala (MA)
A transformable MA Scirocco built aboard the Jupitris. Huge thrusters, made to escape Jupiter's gravity, give it great speed. Its many built-in guns give it strong firepower.
#65 Asshimar
The MS mode of a transformable mobile armor built by the Federation. It flies in the atmosphere by turning into a disc-shaped lifting body. In Zeta Gundam, Buran Blutarch flew it, troubling Kamille's Mk-II on Earth and downing Roberto's Rick Dias. It was later mass-produced and deployed around Dakar.
#66 Asshimar (MA)
A transformable Federation MA with thick armor, but briefly exposed as its chest closes when it turns into an MS.
#67 Gaplant
The MS mode of a transformable mobile armor built by the Federation. Known for the binders on its arms with built-in mega particle cannons. They double as active thruster units, giving it amazing mobility.
#68 Gaplant (MA)
A transformable mobile armor built by the Federation. Designed for space, it wears a large booster for long trips. Its brutal G-forces meant only a few, like Yazan and the Cyber Newtype Rosamia, flew it. Its flaw is a blind spot below its all-around monitor. When docked, its legs unfold as landing gear.
#69 Gabthley
A transformable MS Scirocco built himself, which turns into a fast mobile armor. Flown by Jerid and Mouar under Scirocco, their firepower, speed and teamwork overwhelmed Kamille's Mk-II. It also has a middle mode with legs spread and MA claws bared.
#70 Gabthley (MA)
The Gabthley's mobile armor form.
Grouping its thrusters at the back gives it high speed. Its legs' movable frame also serves as claws for close combat.
#71 Hambrabi
A Titans transformable MS, simpler to transform and build than the Gabthley. Known for five monoeyes and a sharp, angular design. With its Sea Snake electric whip, it excels at close-range team attacks. Yazan, Ramsus and Dunkel flew it against the AEUG, knocking Quattro's Hyaku Shiki into Earth's gravity at the edge of the atmosphere. Its beam rifle is the same Feyadeen Rifle as the Gabthley's.
#72 Hambrabi (MA)
The Hambrabi's mobile armor form. A simple change, but it gains speed, and its arms double as claws for close combat. Its shoulder monoeyes are used in this form.
#73 Byarlant
A Federation MS that flies in the atmosphere with no sub-flight system. With new thrusters, it works in space too.
#74 Palace Athene
A heavy MS Scirocco built himself. It carries many heavy weapons for anti-ship combat, but loses agility when fully armed. Reccoa, who left for Scirocco, flew it in a fight to the death with Emma's Mk-II.
#75 Baund Doc
A transformable Federation MS with a psycommu, mainly used to control the unit, tuned at the Augusta Newtype Lab. Gates Capa flew the silver Unit 1, Rosamia Badam the red Unit 2, and Jerid the yellow Unit 3 (though in the end he seems to have flown Unit 2).
#76 Baund Doc (MA)
The Baund Doc's mobile armor form. It has tough armor and speed. Its MS legs also serve as claws for close combat. The underside, where the MS upper body is exposed, seems to be its weak point.
#77 Psyco Gundam
A Newtype MS built by the Murasame Lab under Federation control. It looks like a Gundam, but its design is based on Zeon's Zeong. It has no psycommu weapons, but a psycommu in its controls aims the beam guns all over its body. Slow and clumsy, its defense and firepower still crush normal mobile suits. Its pilot is the Lab's Cyber Newtype Four Murasame, who faces Kamille in Hong Kong and at Kilimanjaro.
#78 Psyco Gundam (MA)
The Psyco Gundam's mobile armor form. Only in this form can it use its Minovsky craft, so it serves mainly for cruising. But it does not gain much speed like other mobile armors. Too big for the Garuda-class transport Thourien, it was towed in this form.
#79 Psyco Gundam Mk-II
A transformable Newtype MS built by the Murasame Lab. As its name says, a development of the Psyco Gundam. With psycommu weapons like wired beam swords and reflector bits, it outguns the original. In the anime Rosamia Badam ("Rosamie") flew it, until Kamille wrecked it by destroying its head. It then fell to Neo Zeon, where Ple Two flew it.
#80 Psyco Gundam Mk-II (MA)
The MA form of the Murasame Lab's transformable Newtype MS. Like the Psyco Gundam it has a Minovsky craft, but this form never appeared in the anime, as it was not used in the atmosphere. After being wrecked at the Gate of Zedan, it fell to Neo Zeon, and in Gundam ZZ, Ple Two sortied in this form to attack the Argama.
#81 The O
A heavy MS Scirocco built for himself. A high-performance unit with experimental features like a bio-sensor, far faster and more agile than its bulky look suggests. For close combat it hides extra arms in the front of its skirt, leaving no blind spots at any range.
#82 Qubeley
A prototype Newtype MS of Neo Zeon, known for its huge, jutting shoulder armor. Built as an MS version of the One Year War Newtype mobile armor Elmeth, it is code-named "Elmeth 2." It carries funnels, psycommu weapons for all-range attacks, and its shoulder binders double as flexible thruster binders for amazing mobility. Flown by the gifted Newtype Haman Karn, it became Neo Zeon's symbol in name and fact.
Haman's is prototype Unit 1; improved Units 2 and 3 (Qubeley Mk-II) and mass-produced units also exist.
#83 Audhumla
A giant Garuda-class transport plane.
In the anime it belongs to Karaba, the AEUG's ground support, captained by ex-White Base crewman Hayato Kobayashi. Sister ships include the Titans' Thourien.
#84 Dogos Gear
A new, large Titans battleship built at Gryps. Used as the Titans flagship, its MS capacity and firepower make it far stronger than older ships.
#85 Alexandria
The Titans' main cruiser. Several sister ships with minor differences exist, like the Hario and Lombardia. It also appears at the end of Gundam 0083, crewed by ex-Albion bridge officers Pasarov and Simon. Perhaps they died with Jamaican when Emma's Super Gundam attacked...
#86 Jupitris
A giant transport of the helium fleet bound for Jupiter. Besides living blocks, it has factory blocks. Scirocco used it to return from Jupiter to the Earth Sphere and stayed on it as his base. Several sister ships seem to exist; one appears in the Gundam ZZ finale.
#87 Gwadan
A giant battleship developed from Zeon's Gwazine-class of the One Year War. True to Gwazine tradition, it is used by Neo Zeon's elite, including Mineva Zabi. It falls short of the Titans' Dogos Gear in firepower, but beats it in range and acceleration.
#88 Musai Kai
A light cruiser of the Republic of Zeon forces (later absorbed by Neo Zeon). An improved Musai, Zeon's mainstay in the One Year War, but only parts of its hull were changed, with almost no gain in performance.
#89 ZZ Gundam
A prototype transformable heavy MS the AEUG had Anaheim Electronics build, completed after the Zeta Gundam under Anaheim's Project Z. Its concept: "an RX-78 and G-Armor in one unit." So it inherits the Zeta's transformation and the RX-78's core block and G-Parts systems, made of a Core Fighter, the Core Top upper body and the Core Base lower body. Each can fly as a fighter, and together they turn into the heavy fighter-bomber G-Fortress.
Built for firepower, it carries many high-output beam weapons, like the forehead high mega cannon. But it burns energy fast, so its operating time is short. It is the peak of the high-output MS of around U.C. 0087. Judau Ashta flies it at the heart of the Gundam Team.
#90 G-Fortress
The ZZ Gundam's heavy fighter-bomber form. Made of the Core Top (the ZZ's A-Parts and a Core Fighter) and Core Base (the C-Parts and a Core Fighter), it is used mainly for long trips. The ZZ was built to do the job of the RX-78 and G-Armor alone, but the G-Fortress lacks the G-Armor's versatility. Yet its firepower is the strongest of around U.C. 0087.
#91 Core Fighter (ZZ)
A small fighter and escape pod that forms the ZZ Gundam's cockpit block. It can also dock with the Core Top (upper body) or Core Base (lower body) to form larger fighters. Flown by Roux, it reached the Argama ahead of the ZZ itself and even fought alone.
#92 Full Armor ZZ Gundam
The ZZ Gundam with extra armor. It can no longer transform, but its armor is stronger, and its already high firepower rises further. To keep its mobility, the body's backpack and more were upgraded to match (called the Enhanced ZZ Gundam). It was sent into the final battle with Neo Zeon. Not to be confused with the FAZZ of Gundam Sentinel.
#93 G-Fortress (FA)
The heavy fighter-bomber form of the Full Armor ZZ Gundam, used mainly for long trips. In reality, the Full Armor ZZ cannot turn into the G-Fortress.
#94 Gaza D
Successor to Neo Zeon's mass-produced Gaza C. It transforms like the Gaza C and shares many parts, but has more firepower. Early in the anime, the Gaza Storm team used the smokescreen Gaza Storm Formation to trouble the inexperienced Judau.
