#258 Mari Sakurano | Mari | Makoto Kosaka
Akira's classmate and Rinkai Academy's idol. Daughter of the Future Engineering Institute's director, a cheerful, clumsy tomboy Akira likes to tease.
She supports Akira in his fight, and finally, wanting only to help him, joins the Cope Lander Team, backing Raideen in the Bluegar.
#259 Chikara Jinguji | Jinguji | Makio Inoue
Leader of the Cope Lander Team, the Future Engineering Institute's combat unit. Nickname: Mister.
He flies the Bluegar to back Raideen. Cool and a bit cynical, he clashed with the rash Akira, but they became best friends. Before the final battle with the Demon Empire, he died ramming the enemy.
#260 Rei Asuka | Rei | Nako Egawa
A member of the Cope Lander Team.
She has precognition and can hear Raideen's inner voice, giving Akira advice no one else can.
Unlike Mari, she is wise and composed, and Mari always suspects her of being close to Akira.
She once ran with a biker gang, seeking something to live for and throw herself into.
#261 Taro Sarumaru | Sarumaru
A member of the Cope Lander Team.
A young genius engineer who designed the Bluegar.
A bit timid and unsuited to battle, he shines in support, upgrading Raideen and finding enemy weak points.
Nickname: "Great Professor Sarumaru."
#262 Daizaburo Higashiyama | Higashiyama
Director of the Future Engineering Institute and father of Mari Sakurano. He built the science fortress Mutropolis against the Demon Empire, and while commanding it, formed the Cope Lander Team to back Raideen.
#263 Prince Sharkin | Sharkin | Osamu Ichikawa
Prince of the Demon Empire.
Noble and gallant, yet a cold man who will do anything for his goals as prince.
His repeated failures anger Barao, and to regain his standing he grows giant and challenges Raideen to a duel.
He once disguised himself as a human transfer student in Akira's class, under the name Kingo Sunaba.
#264 Barao | Barao
The Great Demon Emperor ruling the Demon Empire.
He sank the continent of Mu into the sea long ago, and plots to bring the Demon Century to the surface.
As a giant stone idol in the Empire's temple, he gives orders to Sharkin and others, and uses magic to revive fossil beasts and punish servants. After Sharkin falls, he reveals his true form and invades the surface himself with the Kyoretsu brothers, Gekido and Gorai.
#265 Berostan | Berostan
Chief of the Demon Empire's priests.
His role is to pray to Barao with spells and revive fossil beasts with his own magic.
He desperately begged Sharkin not to sortie himself against Raideen, fearing for his safety.
#266 Priest | Priest
Priests who hold the rites to revive fossil beasts with Berostan.
#267 Noriko Takaya | Noriko | Noriko Hidaka
A graduate of the Okinawa Girls' Space High School ("Okijo"), run by the Space Force, and a member of Top Squadron. Her father was Admiral Yuzo Takaya, captain of the deep-space explorer Luxion.
A clumsy, upbeat, sporty otaku girl, so athletic she can run 50 laps on foot, hard even for machine weapons. Ohta's keen eye made her a surprise pick for Top Squadron, but in her first space battle she lost Smith Toren, an Exelion pilot she had a crush on, and the shock kept her from fighting in space. She overcame even that, and with Coach's guts and effort, grew into the pilot of Earth's trump card, Gunbuster. Her hobbies are many: anime magazines, posters and model kits.
#268 Kazumi Amano | Kazumi | Rei Sakuma
A Top Squadron member from Okijo, like Noriko. Called "the Queen of Roses," her classmates call her "Onee-sama."
Intelligent and elegant, but given to strong convictions. She never rests on her natural talent, always working hard. She loved Coach Koichiro Ohta and later married him. After his death she taught at Okijo, but in the final battle with the Space Monsters, she boards Gunbuster with Noriko again.
#269 Jung Freud | Jung | Maria Kawamura
An expert pilot raised on the Moon, called "the genius of space combat." She challenged Kazumi to a duel the moment she reached space, then acted all smiles afterward; her mood swings are so wild she almost seems two people.
She is harsh about combat, and once gave up on Noriko when fear nearly crushed her. After Kazumi married Coach, she paired with Noriko as Gunbuster's pilot, but gave up her seat to Kazumi for the final battle. After episode 6 she seems to have become President of the Earth Empire, keeping her last promise to Noriko 12,000 years later.
#270 Tatsumi Tashiro | Tashiro | Tamio Oki
Admiral of the Exelion fleet. Usually a smiling, kindly old man, but grim-faced when commanding in battle. Deeply responsible, he will fight to the death, even self-destruct or ram, to wipe out the Space Monsters. His catchphrase is "Good grief!" and he loves sushi. Off duty, he is usually seen eating.
#271 Koichiro Ohta | Ohta | Norio Wakamoto
Head of the RX Project and Top Squadron's leader. A demon coach whose motto is "guts and effort," he trains Noriko brutally. A survivor of the Luxion, lost to Space Monsters, he blames himself for her father's death.
Dying of space radiation sickness, he uses his last days to finish Gunbuster, leaving all to Noriko and Kazumi. He marries Kazumi and dies about a year later.
#272 Vice Captain | Vice Captain | Tomomichi Nishimura
The Exelion's vice captain. So scientific Tashiro says "Your galaxy must be full of numbers." He reports the facts plainly, even at the worst times.
#273 Kimiko Higuchi | Kimiko
Noriko's best friend and Okijo classmate, always the straight man to carefree Noriko. She loves astrology. She later married and had a child, and meets Noriko again when she returns after 10 years...
#274 Shinji Ikari | Shinji | Megumi Ogata
Pilot of Eva Unit-01.
The Third Child, chosen by the Marduk Institute. He lost his mother when little and lived apart from his father Gendo for over 10 years, until he was called to Tokyo-3 because of the Angel attacks. An introvert, he shies from contact with others. At first he pilots Eva out of need and duty, but finds its meaning through battle. Around NERV and other kids he slowly opens up, but events in the war wound his heart.
Playing the cello is his only skill.
#275 Dummy Plug | Dummy Plug | Megumi Hayashibara
When Eva Unit-01 is downed, its pilot switches to this.
Strictly speaking, though, it is not the Dummy Plug that pilots Unit-01 when it goes berserk.
#276 Rei Ayanami | Rei | Megumi Hayashibara
Pilot of Eva Unit-00.
The First Child, first pilot of the Eva series.
Her past is a total mystery; a quiet girl who never shows emotion. She says she pilots Eva for "bonds."
She has special feelings for Gendo, and treasures the glasses he dropped when he pulled her from the entry plug during a Unit-00 test. Her secret is tied closely to the making of the Evas and Gendo's goals.
#277 Asuka Langley Soryu | Asuka | Yuko Miyamura
Pilot of Eva Unit-02.
The Second Child, chosen by the Marduk Institute. A genius who is a quarter Japanese, a quarter German, and graduated college at 14.
Proud and sure of her skills, she looks down on others, especially boys her age. This seems rooted in her mother, who took part in Eva experiments and killed herself after mental contamination. Finding her worth only as an Eva pilot, her mind begins to break once she cannot accept losing to Shinji.
#278 Toji Suzuhara | Toji | Tomokazu Seki
Shinji's classmate.
A hot-blooded boy who speaks Kansai dialect and dotes on his sister.
When his sister was hurt in an Eva battle, he took it out on Shinji, but seeing Shinji fight in earnest, he became his friend, like Kensuke.
He was later chosen as the Fourth Child by the Marduk Institute to pilot Eva Unit-03. But an Angel took over Unit-03 during its activation test, and Unit-01 on the Dummy Plug defeated it, leaving Toji badly hurt.
#279 Gendo Ikari | Gendo
NERV's Supreme Commander, once director of the Artificial Evolution Lab and of Gehirn.
Charged with studying and destroying the Angels, he alone runs the Human Instrumentality Project.
A cold man who will do anything for his goals, he is always overbearing, even to his men and son, with one exception: Rei Ayanami.
His vision of Instrumentality differs from SEELE's, and in the end he turns against SEELE.
#280 Kozo Fuyutsuki | Fuyutsuki
NERV's Sub-Commander.
Always calm, and a bit sarcastic.
He has known Gendo since he was an associate professor.
His first impression of Gendo was "an unpleasant man," but after Second Impact he became his ally.
He sometimes disagrees with Gendo, but is one of the few who understand his goals.
#281 Misato Katsuragi | Misato
Operations Chief of NERV HQ's Tactical Operations Division. Rank: captain (later major). Her main job is planning and leading operations against the Angels.
As a girl she was at the site of Second Impact, where she lost her father, and joined NERV out of hatred for the Angels.
On the surface she is a cheerful, sloppy, friendly big sister, and slowly opens Shinji's heart while living with him. But she carries scars from her past.
She has been Ritsuko's friend since college, and Kaji's lover back then.
She loves beer, sometimes drinking in daytime, and keeps a hot spring penguin, Pen Pen.
#282 Ritsuko Akagi | Ritsuko
Head of NERV HQ's Technology Development Division.
Daughter of Dr. Naoko Akagi, who laid the theory of the MAGI supercomputers, she leads Project E, the Eva development project.
Cool-headed, she can be cold to others when facing facts.
Misato's friend since college.
In contrast to her cool nature, she collects cat trinkets and keeps a cat at her family home.
She is also Gendo's lover.
#283 Ryoji Kaji | Kaji
A member of NERV's Special Inspection Division.
He comes to Japan from the German branch escorting Asuka, Unit-02 and a mysterious object called Adam. He is in fact a triple agent for NERV, SEELE and the Japanese Ministry of Home Affairs, and is assassinated when exposed.
He seems a flirt who chats up any woman he likes, but Asuka adores him, and he is a caring big brother to the young. He grows watermelons.
He and Misato were lovers in college.
#284 Shigeru Aoba | Aoba
A bridge operator in NERV HQ's Central Command, handling comms and intel analysis.
When the JSSDF stormed NERV HQ, he cheered Maya on and fought back with a gun; laid-back in speech but brave. He plays guitar.
#285 Makoto Hyuga | Hyuga
A member of NERV HQ's Operations Division who assists Misato in planning and analyzes the battle as an operator.
His feelings for Misato go beyond respect, and he gets her top-secret data even at risk to his own position.
#286 Maya Ibuki | Maya
Ritsuko's subordinate in NERV HQ's Technology Division, an operator in Central Command who monitors the Eva pilots.
She admires her boss Ritsuko.
Girlish and fastidious, she hates using any means necessary.
#287 Kensuke Aida | Kensuke
Shinji's classmate.
He, Shinji and Toji are called "the Three Stooges."
Toji's best friend from before Shinji arrived. A military otaku interested in the Evas, he even begged Misato to let him pilot one. He always carries a video camera, gleefully filming any weapons he finds.
#288 Hikari Horaki | Hikari
Shinji's classmate and class rep, a model student called "Class Rep" by all.
She secretly likes Toji, and promises to make him a boxed lunch since he eats store-bought bread. But the Unit-03 incident means she never keeps that promise in the story.
She was drawn to his kindness. She has an older sister, Kodama, and a younger one, Nozomi.
#289 SEELE | SEELE
Members of the secret group behind the Human Instrumentality Project. They appear only as monolith holograms and voices.
#290 Keel Lorenz | Keel
The central figure of the Instrumentality Committee and SEELE. He seems to have long known Gendo.
He always wears a visor, and his face is never shown.
#291 Kaworu Nagisa | Kaworu | Akira Ishida
The Fifth Child, sent by SEELE. Like Rei Ayanami, his past is a total mystery. He can change his sync ratio at will, astonishing the NERV staff.
He reaches out to Shinji often, and his honesty and kindness open Shinji's heart.
But he is Tabris, the 17th Angel sent by SEELE, and he invades Terminal Dogma with the unmanned Unit-02.
He died by his own wish, crushed in the hand of Shinji's Unit-01.
He loved song, and hummed Beethoven's Ninth.
