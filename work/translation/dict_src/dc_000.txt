#1 Irmgard Kazahara | Irm | Kenyu Horiuchi
Known as Irm. He is actually a lieutenant of the EFF Far East Branch and a test pilot for the SRX Project's new machines.
A cultured man, smooth with words and hands, and a ladies' man with a wide age range. But that is only a facade; he can be ruthless when it comes to his own goals.
He was once a member of the PTX Team, the anti-alien special unit that preceded the SRX Team. It was led by Ingram Prisken, and also included Lt. Ring Mao (now president of Mao Industries).
Irm was also the first human to make contact with Cybuster on the surface. His father is Dr. Jonathan Kazahara, who designed the Grungust at the Tesla Leicht Institute with Robert Ohmiya.
#2 Ring Mao | Ring
President of Mao Industries, maker of Personal Troopers. Her father, Ting Mao, founded the company. Half Japanese, half Chinese, she is a cool rationalist. She detests men and strives never to lose to them, trying to keep a historical view beyond what is in front of her. Inspired by her father, she became a PT pilot and served in the EFF Far East Branch's PTX Team. After her father died in a mysterious accident, she left the military and took over Mao Industries. She now supports the SRX Project, helping develop the R Series and other machines. The company's troublesome PTs keep her busy, but her piloting is far from rusty. She and Irmgard Kazahara were lovers in the PTX days, though she acts as if she regrets it. She is 28 years old.
#3 Sleggar Law | Sleggar | Makio Inoue
A pilot assigned to the White Base when it left Jaburo for space. His rank is lieutenant. He seems crude and often speaks like an outsider, but he is sensitive at heart, one of the few adults aboard.
After advising Mirai as she wavered over her fiance Cameron, the two grew close. Before the attack on Solomon he left his mother's ring with her. He died in a desperate attack on Dozle's Big Zam.
#4 Matilda Ajan | Matilda
Lieutenant commanding the Federation's Medea supply unit. She delivered power-up parts for the Gundam to the White Base. When the Black Tri-Stars launched their Jet Stream Attack on the Gundam, she cut in with her Medea, and her cockpit was crushed. Though her time with the White Base crew was short, her beauty and dignity made her the idol of many men aboard. She was to marry Lt. Woody of Jaburo after her mission.
#5 Gihren Zabi | Gihren | Banjo Ginga
Eldest Zabi son and de facto ruler of the Principality of Zeon. With an IQ of 240, he is an elitist and a dictator. A cold strategist who used even his brother Garma's death to rouse the war effort. He killed Degwin and a Federation fleet with the Solar Ray to take full power, but was killed by his rival and sister Kycilia at A Baoa Qu.
#6 Kycilia Zabi | Kycilia | Mami Koyama
Eldest daughter of the Zabi family, commander of Zeon's Mobile Assault Force. Rank: rear admiral.
A skilled politician, she is her brother Gihren's political rival.
She recognized Newtypes early, and knew that Char was the son of Zeon Zum Deikun, founder of the Principality.
She seized power by killing Gihren, who had murdered their father Degwin, but was shot by Char as his final revenge on the Zabis while fleeing a doomed A Baoa Qu.
#7 Dozle Zabi | Dozle | Daisuke Gori
Third son of the Zabi family and commander of Zeon's Space Attack Force. Rank: vice admiral.
Unlike Gihren and Kycilia, he is a pure warrior. His facial scar is said to come from a terrorist bomb aimed at the Zabis.
Bold, open-hearted and honest, he cares for his men and his family, and his troops trust him deeply.
He commanded the defense of Solomon and sortied in the mobile armor Big Zam himself, but was shot down by Amuro's Gundam after Sleggar's suicide attack opened the way.
He has a daughter, Mineva, with his wife Zenna.
#8 M'Quve | M'Quve | Kaneto Shiozawa
An officer of Zeon's Mobile Space Force, directly under Kycilia. Rank: colonel. He ran Zeon's key mining bases on Earth, then fought in space after losing Operation Odessa. Cunning and unscrupulous, he used an H-bomb banned by the Antarctic Treaty. He piloted the prototype mobile suit Gyan, but lost to Amuro's Gundam. He collects antiques, and even in his last moments he worried about a vase meant for Kycilia.
#9 Ramba Ral | Ral | Masashi Hirose
An officer of Zeon's Strategic Space Force. Rank: captain. Ordered by Dozle to pursue the White Base to avenge Garma, he descends to Earth.
Known as "the Blue Giant," he is a battle-hardened hero who gives his all to his mission. Sharp judgment, first-rate piloting and a bold, caring nature earn him his men's trust. His father, Jinba Ral, served Zeon Zum Deikun, and he had met Zeon's daughter, Sayla.
He pushed the Gundam hard in his Gouf, and also met Amuro after the boy fled the White Base, and showed him his stature as a man. Amuro came to see him as someone to surpass, and he greatly shaped the boy's growth.
After the Gundam destroyed his Gouf, he fought on foot and finally took his own life.
His common-law wife, Hamon, traveled with his unit.
#10 Crowley Hamon | Hamon | Yumi Nakatani
Ramba Ral's common-law wife, who joins his pursuit of the White Base. Though not a soldier, she assists Ral and is adored by his men.
She meets Amuro, a runaway from the White Base, in a desert town and takes an instant liking to him.
After Ral's death she leads the remaining troops against the White Base, but dies one step short of victory when Ryu Jose rams her.
#11 Clamp | Clamp | Kaneto Shiozawa
Ramba Ral's second-in-command.
A guerrilla expert, he leads the 2nd team in a boarding raid on the White Base.
Shocked to find children aboard, he dies in the firefight.
#12 Cozun Graham | Cozun | Koji Totani
One of Ramba Ral's men. Rank: ensign. Captured by the White Base, he tries to escape using a bomb hidden in a false tooth, but is shot from behind.
#13 Acous | Acous | Hirotaka Suzuoki
One of Ramba Ral's men. He flies a Zaku with Cozun in formation with Ral's Gouf.
In the first clash with the White Base, he harries the Gundam with Cracker grenades, but is shot down by Amuro's Guncannon on his second sortie.
#14 Challia Bull | Challia | Shojiro Kihara
Once part of Zeon's Jupiter fleet, his Newtype abilities led Gihren to send him to Kycilia. Rank: captain.
Aware that his powers are a tool of political scheming, he told Char of the future of Newtypes.
He showed his powers in the mobile armor Braw Bro, but was shot down by Amuro's Gundam.
#15 Gaia | Gaia | Issei Masamune
A pilot of Zeon's Special Mobile Force.
Leader of the "Black Tri-Stars" with Mash and Ortega, he has been one of Zeon's aces since the Battle of Loum, with confidence to match.
Flying a Dom, he leads the three-unit Jet Stream Attack.
After Mash fell, he sought revenge, but was shot down by the Gundam late in Operation Odessa.
#16 Ortega | Ortega | Issei Futamata
A pilot of Zeon's Special Mobile Force.
One of the "Black Tri-Stars" with Gaia and Mash, a Zeon ace since the Battle of Loum.
Flying a Dom, he brings up the rear of the three-unit Jet Stream Attack.
He closed in on the Gundam after it downed Mash, but crushed the cockpit of Matilda's Medea when it cut in.
Like Gaia, he hunted the Gundam from then on, and was shot down late in Operation Odessa.
#17 Mash | Mash | Masaharu Sato
A pilot of Zeon's Special Mobile Force.
One of the "Black Tri-Stars" with Gaia and Ortega, a Zeon ace since the Battle of Loum.
Flying a Dom, he is second in the three-unit Jet Stream Attack, but is shot down by Amuro's Gundam, which used Gaia's Dom as a stepping stone.
#18 Conscon | Conscon | Osamu Kato
An officer of Zeon's Strategic Space Force.
Rank: rear admiral.
Dozle orders him to hunt the White Base, which Char has failed to stop, to prove the incompetence of Kycilia's protege. But his twelve Rick Doms and his heavy cruiser Chivvay are wiped out in mere minutes.
#19 Tokwan | Tokwan | Issei Masamune
A pilot assigned to Char after he went into space. Rank: ensign.
Known for the skull on his helmet.
As test pilot of the mobile armor Bygro, he came within a step of beating the Gundam, but was shot down.
His subordinate Dimitri later sought revenge on the Gundam, proof of how his men trusted him.
#20 Christina Mackenzie | Chris | Megumi Hayashibara
A Federation pilot born in Side 6. Rank: lieutenant. A member of the team developing the Newtype Gundam NT-1 Alex. Raised in a fairly well-off family, she is gentle, the "girl next door" type. Her job is to give untested machines the combat skills of a veteran pilot, a role called "shoe fitter." Short on staff, she was also the Alex's test pilot. She repelled the Cyclops Team, who came to steal the Alex, in the Alex with Chobham Armor. A week later she fought Bernie's Zaku Kai, and destroyed it though her machine was wrecked. She never learned who piloted that Zaku Kai.
#21 Bernard Wiseman | Bernie | Koji Tsujitani
Drafted upon finishing high school, he becomes a Zeon MS pilot. Rank: corporal. After his first battle, he joins Steiner's Cyclops Team as a replacement. In the raid to steal the NT-1 Alex in Side 6, his mistake dooms the mission and the rest of the team is killed. Around that time he grows close to Christina Mackenzie and to Alfred Izuruha (Al), a boy of the colony.
When Zeon command decides to destroy the entire colony if the Alex cannot be taken, he sets out to stop it by defeating the Alex, with Al's support. He wrecks the Alex, but dies when its beam saber pierces his cockpit...
#22 Steiner Hardy | Steiner | Yosuke Akimoto
Captain and leader of the Cyclops Team, a Zeon special forces unit. He infiltrates Side 6 to steal the new NT-1 Alex, but fails due to Bernie's mistake. A professional soldier who does his duty knowing his team is expendable, sent only to confirm the new Gundam. He looks mild, but is ruthless enough to use the boy Al, and is also a first-class veteran MS pilot.
#23 Kou Uraki | Kou | Ryo Horikawa
A test pilot training at Torrington Base in Australia. Rank: ensign. He sees Gato steal the Gundam GP02 before his eyes and volunteers to retake it. Well-versed in mobile suits, he surprises Nina by spotting at a glance that the GP02 carries a nuke. In his first battle Gato sees through his inexperience and humbles him, but Kou's talent blooms until he can fight Gato as an equal. He was shy even for Neumann Academy, known for its sheltered boys, and Nina led the way in their relationship. He hates carrots.
#24 Chuck Keith | Keith | Yoshiharu Yamada
A Neumann Academy classmate of Kou who trained with him at Torrington Base. Rank: ensign. He and his best friend Kou witness Gato steal the GP02. Cheerful if a bit timid, his vitality carries him through the war with the Delaz Fleet. He first hit on the beautiful Nina, but hit it off with the mechanic Mora instead.
#25 South Burning | Burning | Masashi Sugawara
A veteran Federation pilot who has led Monsha and the others since the One Year War. Rank: captain. After the GP02 theft he commands the MS team of the Albion pursuit unit. During training in space he runs into Cima's unit and drives it off, but his damaged machine explodes on the way back, killing him. Strict but warm, he was loved by every pilot on the Albion. He has a wife on Earth, living apart from him.
#26 Bernard Monsha | Monsha | Chafurin
A veteran Federation MS pilot of the One Year War. Rank: lieutenant. He joins the Albion as a replacement pilot to chase the GP02 stolen by Gato. But the arrogance common to skilled pilots causes constant trouble with the crew. Still, he respects his superior, Capt. Burning, and in the end sees Kou, whom he first called an amateur, as a comrade.
#27 Nina Purpleton | Nina | Rei Sakuma
An Anaheim Electronics systems engineer and a Lunarian (Moon-born). Part of the Gundam Development Project, she is on loan to the Federation. She witnesses the GP02 theft at Torrington Base, and stays with the Albion until the conflict ends. Drawn to Kou and his talent, she is torn upon learning that his enemy is her former lover, Gato.
#28 Mora Bascht | Mora
A Federation mechanic and lieutenant. She joins the Albion as chief mechanic for all the Gundam prototypes. Bigger than most men and bold, she is the crew's big sister. She is Nina's confidant as Nina struggles with Kou, and grows close to Keith.
#29 John Kowen | Kowen
Federation lieutenant general in charge of the Gundam Development Project. A skilled strategist, one of the few sane leaders in a corrupt military. From Jaburo, he backs the Albion unit sent to retake the GP02. He has a faction of his own, but is finally forced out by the schemes of Bask and others.
