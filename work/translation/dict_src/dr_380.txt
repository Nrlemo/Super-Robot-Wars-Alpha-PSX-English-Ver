#380 VF-1J Valkyrie B
The ground combat form of the VF-1J Valkyrie. In the TV series, the first unit Hikaru flew after joining the U.N. Forces. Seen in the opening and eyecatches, it is likely the type viewers of the time remember best. It is also the earliest Valkyrie type, but probably fewer were built than the standard A type.
#381 VF-1J Armored Valkyrie
A VF-1 fitted with the Protector Weapon System to cover the series' weak firepower and armor. It cannot transform while armored, but hits harder. The protectors are easily shed for escape in an emergency. Like the Super Pack, it was standard Valkyrie gear for all units. Hikaru used it in TV episode 9, "Miss Macross"; the movie shows it in one scene.

#397 VT-1 Valkyrie F
A two-seat Valkyrie for training. It can take GERWALK form, but lacking arms, it just extends its legs. Extra tanks all over save fuel wasted by clumsy trainees. #398 Destroid Monster
The largest, best-armed Destroid, the Macross's ground weapons; a non-orbital land unit. Built as a walking land battleship, it is too slow to fight alone. But its firepower exceeds a strategic bombing. It needs a crew of three: pilot, gunner and controller.

#414 YF-19 Fighter
A next-generation all-environment fighter (ADVANCED VARIABLE FIGHTER) built by Shinsei Industry. Code name: Excalibur; a two-seater. A development of the VF-11, a direct descendant of the VF-1, it adopts breakthroughs like super AI avionics and standard pinpoint barriers. It climbs superbly, reaching its aerodynamic ceiling in 48 seconds. It can serve alone as an anti-ship attacker, and against mobile weapons it far outclasses older units. It can also mount Fast Packs, extra arms, and a fold booster, though it can fold only about 20 light-years (one way).

#417 YF-21 Fighter
A next-generation all-environment fighter built by General Galaxy. Code name: Sturm Vogel. It uses active stealth and BDI brain-wave control for flight and fire control; the pilot syncs body with machine and controls all by brain waves. Its wings of new material change their shape and area as needed. Even normally it matches the YF-19, and with its limiter off and its arms and legs, useless in Fighter mode, discarded, it gets even faster. But then neither machine nor pilot can bear the G-forces. In the anime, Guld removed the limiter against the unmanned stealth fighter Ghost X-9 and downed it at the cost of his life.
