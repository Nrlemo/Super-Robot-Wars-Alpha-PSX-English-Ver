#30 Anavel Gato | Gato | Akio Otsuka
A Zeon ace since the One Year War. Rank: major. His great feats in his Gelgoog at Solomon earned him the name "the Nightmare of Solomon," and he even appears in Federation academy textbooks. Devoted to Zeon's ideals, he joined the Delaz Fleet after the war. He was central to Operation Stardust and did much to carry it out. But amid the chaos of Cima's betrayal, while joining the Axis fleet, he rammed a Federation battleship and died in space.
#31 Kelley Layzner | Kelley | Tessho Genda
Gato's old comrade, a Zeon captain who retired after losing an arm in the war. He runs a junk shop in Von Braun City, secretly restoring the mobile armor Val Walo to join the Delaz Fleet.
Later he met Kou, who had left the military in despair. Repaying Kelley's hospitality, Kou helped finish the Val Walo, knowing he was Zeon. But when Kelley learned his contact Cima only wanted the Val Walo, the deal fell through. To live and die as a warrior, he challenged Kou's GP01Fb on the lunar surface, but lost to Kou's daring tactics.
#32 Cima Garahau | Cima | Mari Mashiba
A female Zeon officer. Rank: commander.
Since the One Year War she has done Zeon's dirty work, such as gassing colonies. Commanding the Zanzibar-class cruiser Lili Marleen, she is a capable fleet leader, but her naked ambition made Gato distrust her. Indeed, while in the Delaz Fleet, she secretly dealt with the Federation and Anaheim, selling out the colony drop to secure her own safety.
In the end she seized Delaz's flagship Gwaden. She meant to hand Delaz to the Federation, but shot him and fled in the Gerbera Tetra. She then met Kou's GP03 and died to a direct hit from its mega beam cannon.
#33 Aguille Delaz | Delaz | Kiyoshi Kobayashi
A zealous follower of Gihren Zabi who waited for the day Zeon would rise again. Three years after the war, he launched Operation Stardust, a colony drop. At the war's end he stopped Gato from a suicidal sortie and took him in. Stardust went as planned, but Cima's betrayal killed him before it was done.
#34 Karius | Karius | Nobuo Tobita
A Zeon sergeant who has served Gato since the One Year War. In the second phase of Operation Stardust, he escorted Gato and the GP02 to Solomon to the very end. In the final operation, at Gato's request, he took Nina, Gato's former lover, to the Axis advance fleet.
#35 Kamille Bidan | Kamille | Nobuo Tobita
A civilian boy from Green Noa 1, a colony in Side 7. His hobbies include karate, Homo Abilis (one-man glider) flying and junior mobile suits. He hates his girlish name, and was arrested by the Titans for punching Jerid, who laughed at it.
Meeting Quattro, who had infiltrated Green Noa, he joined the AEUG. He fought in the Gundam Mk-II on his first try, leading Bright and others to call him "Amuro Ray reborn." With the AEUG he won many victories, becoming an ace as his Newtype powers awakened. He also had a talent for MS design; the Mk-II's Flying Armor and the Zeta Gundam include his ideas.
His personality is complex: naive yet violent, boyishly honest yet calculating. At first he fought out of vague anger at the Titans, but meeting Quattro, Amuro and Four, he came to fight by his own will to end the war, and grew greatly as a person.
He defeated Scirocco at the Colony Laser, but his mind was broken. He then recovered with Fa, yet still guided Judau and Ple at times in Gundam ZZ, and recovered in its final episode.
#36 Quattro Bajeena | Quattro | Shuichi Ikeda
Leader of the Argama's MS team. Only a captain, he flies the Rick Dias and Hyaku Shiki and advises on strategy, a key AEUG figure. He always wears sunglasses, and his words and past are shrouded in mystery. He is really Char Aznable, Zeon's Red Comet, sent by Axis to scout the Earth Sphere. He noticed Kamille's Newtype potential early and looked after him in many ways. At first he stayed a mere pilot, fighting the Titans who threatened the Spacenoids. But after meeting his old rival Amuro again, the rise of Scirocco, Blex's death and his Dakar speech, he became the AEUG's leader. After his battle with Haman's Qubeley at the Colony Laser, he vanished. Char never used the name Quattro again. "Quattro" is Italian for "four," marking his fourth name after Casval, Edouard and Char.
#37 Emma Sheen | Emma | Maya Okamoto
Once a Titans member, she joined the AEUG out of conviction. Rank: lieutenant. Smart, competitive and strong-willed, she acts as a big sister to Kamille, Katz and Fa. Henken was sweet on her, and she did not seem to mind. Gravely wounded fighting Reccoa, who had left for Scirocco, she died with Kamille at her side.
#38 Apolly | Apolly | Hiroyuki Shibamoto
A pilot who joins the AEUG with Quattro. Rank: lieutenant. One of Char's men since his Zeon days, he flies a Rick Dias and backs Quattro in battle with his comrade Roberto. As a veteran, he sometimes led the Argama's MS team. At the Gate of Zedan he shielded Fa and was shot down by Jerid.
#39 Roberto | Roberto | Kozo Shioya
A pilot who joins the AEUG with Quattro. Rank: lieutenant. One of Char's men since his Zeon days, he flies a Rick Dias and backs Quattro in battle with Apolly. After descending for the Jaburo raid, he took on Buran's Asshimar so Apolly and Quattro could reach space, but was shot down.
#40 Henken Bekkener | Henken | Jurota Kosugi
An AEUG member and the Argama's first captain.
After Bright took over, he captained the Radish, supporting the AEUG flagship Argama. In the One Year War he captained a Federation Salamis-class ship; at A Baoa Qu he never met the Zeong, but felt the Red Comet's pressure.
Frank and friendly, he fell for Emma and made several clumsy advances. He died using his ship as a shield to protect her, perishing once he knew she was safe.
#41 Blex Forer | Blex
Leader of the anti-Federation group AEUG.
A Federation general who formed the AEUG with allies inside and out of the military to oppose the Titans. Assassinated before his goal was met, he left his cause to Quattro.
#42 Fa Yuiry | Fa | Miyuki Matsuoka
Kamille's next-door neighbor in Green Noa 1. After the Titans seized her parents, she joined the AEUG as a pilot. In Green Noa she fussed over Kamille out of affection, but in the AEUG fear and anxiety made her lean on him. Wanting his approval, she pushed herself too hard as a pilot more than once. Early in Gundam ZZ she leaves the Argama to care for Kamille, whose mind broke in the final battle with Scirocco.
#43 Katz Kobayashi | Katz | Keiichi Nanba
One of the children aboard the White Base in the One Year War, later adopted by Hayato and Fraw. He persuaded the confined Amuro to join the AEUG with him. Idolizing Amuro and seeing Kamille as a rival, he kept acting recklessly and was often disciplined for it. He fell for Sarah and tried to free her from Scirocco, but shot her down by mistake when she shielded him. Just before the final battle with the Titans, he crashed into a rock and was killed by Yazan.
#44 Torres | Torres | Hiroyuki Shibamoto
An Argama bridge officer in charge of deck control, comms and search. Close in age to Kamille, the two were friends.
In Gundam ZZ he even piloted due to a lack of pilots. He stayed aboard after Judau's group moved to the Nahel Argama, assisting acting captain Beecha.
#45 Saegusa | Saegusa | Kozo Shioya
An Argama bridge officer handling navigation and helm. Quiet and unassuming.
#46 Beltorchika Irma | Beltorchika
A member of Karaba, the AEUG's ground support group. She handles liaison and diversions, but boldly goes to the front, and can seem tactless. She rallied Amuro, who had joined the AEUG but could not bring himself to fight, and played a big part in his comeback. In the Dakar assembly raid she was on the front line, broadcasting Quattro's speech. By then she had mellowed, even chatting with Kamille. She first appeared flying her Beechcraft 17 biplane.
#47 Hayato Kobayashi | Hayato
A former White Base crewman, now captain of Karaba's Audhumla, directing the front. In the One Year War he piloted the Guntank and Guncannon. After the war he married Fraw Bow, adopted Katz, Retz and Kikka, and ran a war museum. But he chose to join Karaba and became one of its leaders.
In Gundam ZZ he dies protecting Judau.
#48 Fraw Kobayashi | Fraw
Amuro's next-door neighbor in Side 7 and the White Base's comm officer. She married Hayato after the war. Visiting Amuro under Federation house arrest with her adopted son Katz, she helped spark his comeback.
#49 Astonaige Medoz | Astonaige
Chief mechanic of the Argama, Nahel Argama and Londo Bell. Highly skilled and a stickler for maintenance, but friendly and good-natured.
#50 Kai Shiden | Kai
A former White Base crewman who piloted the Guncannon in the One Year War. After it he left the military to become a freelance journalist.
A cynic who was not much of a team player, he found a clear purpose after meeting the spy Miharu Ratokie in the war. As a journalist he kept that resolve, infiltrating Jaburo to probe the Titans on his own. Sharp and perceptive, he left Hayato a letter warning him about Char, who was hiding his identity.
#51 Kigunan | Kigunan
A former Zeon soldier sent with Quattro to scout the Earth Sphere. He calls Quattro "Colonel," his old Zeon rank, and feeds him information privately.
#52 Sayla Mass | Sayla | Yo Inoue
Daughter of Zeon Zum Deikun, founder of the Principality of Zeon; her real name is Artesia Som Deikun. A medical student in Side 7, she boarded the White Base after being caught in the Zeon attack. She later ran a business on Earth. In the Gundam ZZ finale she appears before Bright with the missing Leina, and tells him that her brother Char will one day return.
#53 Four Murasame | Four | Saeko Shimazu
The fourth Cyber Newtype of the Murasame Lab. She joins the Audhumla pursuit unit with the Psyco Gundam and meets Kamille in Hong Kong. They grow close, but told the Lab will restore her memories if she beats the AEUG, she fights him in the Psyco Gundam. She breaks free of its hold once and sacrifices herself to send Kamille into space.
She meets him again at the battle for Kilimanjaro Base, but has been re-conditioned and faces him as an enemy. She comes to her senses, but dies shielding his Zeta from Jerid. Her life and death helped Kamille grow.
#54 Jerid Messa | Jerid | Kazuhiko Inoue
A Titans officer. Rank: lieutenant. Proud of his elite status, he tends to look down on others. He laughed at Kamille's name, sparking trouble, and through Bask's scheme killed Kamille's mother Hilda. After losing comrades and his lover to Kamille, he hunts him as his sworn enemy.
He finally challenged Kamille in the Baund Doc, but died in the blast of Henken's Radish.
#55 Mouar Pharaoh | Mouar | Yoshiko Sakakibara
A female Titans officer who helped Jerid escape Jaburo and stayed with him after. Under Scirocco, she teams with Jerid in a Gabthley. Scirocco saw promise in her, but she chose to fight beside Jerid, whom she loved. She died shielding him from Kamille's Zeta.
#56 Kacricon Cacooler | Kacricon | Koji Totani
A Titans officer. Rank: lieutenant. Jerid's good friend and frequent partner. More mature than Jerid, and quite clever too. During atmospheric entry in the Jaburo drop, he challenged Kamille's Mk-II with Flying Armor, but died when his ballute was torn.
#57 Lila Milla Rira | Lila | Kimie Sawaki
A veteran pilot of the Luna II garrison. She dislikes the Titans, but helps Jerid chase the Argama. Seeing him swallow his pride to ask for her guidance, she sees the makings of "a good man" in him and teaches him to fight in space. She met Kamille and Quattro at 30 Bunch in Side 1, site of a gas massacre, and was then shot down by Kamille's Mk-II. Dying, she grasped Oldtypes and Newtypes, and her spirit advised Kamille via the Zeta in his battle with Scirocco.
#58 Bask Om | Bask | Daisuke Gori
A Titans officer who commands at the front. Rank: colonel. The very embodiment of Titans elitism, he does not shrink from atrocities like gas massacres against resistance. He scorns even non-Titans Federation troops, and struck Bright for questioning his tactics. But he is short-sighted, and fell into the trap of Scirocco, who planned to take over the Titans. Reccoa's Palace Athene shot through the Dogos Gear's bridge, killing him. He also appears in Gundam 0083, ready to meet the Delaz Fleet's colony drop with the Solar System.
#59 Jamaican Daninghan | Jamaican | Keaton Yamada
A Titans officer and Bask's right-hand man.
Rank: major. An elitist like Bask, he runs brutal operations like dropping a derelict colony on Granada. A coward at heart, he is no great commander, and the Alexandria's captain Gady secretly despised him. He fell into Yazan's trap, and Emma's Super Gundam shot through his bridge, killing him.
#60 Gady Kinsey | Gady | Koji Totani
A Titans officer and captain of the Alexandria. A capable man, he was glad to finally use the ship as he saw fit after Jamaican, its fleet commander, died. In the final battle he died with his ship when the AEUG fired the Colony Laser.
#61 Buran Blutarch | Buran | Hidetoshi Nakamura
Commander of the Thourien. Rank: major. A Federation regular, he dislikes the Titans, and loathes Cyber Newtypes. Yet he shows concern for Rosamia herself, and his men trust him. A first-rate pilot, he shot down Roberto in his Asshimar, but lost to the teamwork of Kamille and a revived Amuro.
