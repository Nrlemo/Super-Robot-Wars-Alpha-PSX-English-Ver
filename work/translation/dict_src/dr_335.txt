Space's immune system: they nest in stars and wipe out a bacteria called intelligent life, the foe of all thinking beings.

Space's immune system: they nest in stars and wipe out a bacteria called intelligent life, the foe of all thinking beings.

Space's immune system: they nest in stars and wipe out a bacteria called intelligent life, the foe of all thinking beings.

#339 EVA-01
Formally the Multipurpose Humanoid Decisive Weapon, Artificial Human Evangelion Unit-01. An anti-Angel weapon built by the special agency NERV over 14 years at vast cost; Unit-01 is the test type. It has an A.T. Field (ABSOLUTE TERROR FIELD), an absolute physical barrier, and is piloted by motherless 14-year-olds called Children. It runs on power fed by an umbilical cable, which can be cut in an emergency (leaving about 5 minutes of activity).
Unit-01, piloted by Shinji Ikari, holds more mysteries than other Evas, like restarting past its limit and going berserk. It has a red sphere called a core (Angels have one too). In the movie, the Spear of Longinus pierces this core and Unit-01 becomes the Tree of Life. In TV episode 19, fighting the 14th Angel Zeruel, it absorbed an S2 (SUPER SOLENOID) Engine, so it should no longer need its umbilical cable.
The Magoroku Exterminate Sword and Counter Sword exist only in design notes, never in the anime.
The Eva's official height is 40.0 to 200.0 m, and weight 700 to 96,000 t.
#340 EVA-01 (Berserk)
EVA-01 gone berserk, a mysterious state seen only in Unit-01. It mostly triggers when all seems lost, beyond even Shinji's control. Berserk, Unit-01 is savage, destroying its foes utterly. It can also regenerate, once restoring a severed arm on its own. In TV episode 18 it seems to have been forced berserk by the Dummy System, modeled on Rei Ayanami's personality.
#341 EVA-00
The world's first Evangelion, a prototype of Units 01, 02 and the rest. It seems it was never meant for combat, so it cannot use most equipment. It went berserk in its activation test and was frozen in special bakelite. Several Unit-00s seem to have been built before one actually activated. The Evas' height and weight were first set when they were cast in the Super Robot Wars series.
#342 EVA-00 Kai
EVA-00, wrecked in Operation Yashima, rebuilt with the same arms as the other Evas and repainted blue. Based on the test-type Unit-00, it rarely fought at the front, and mostly gave rear support. In the anime it was destroyed fighting the 16th Angel Armisael and left scrapped.
#343 EVA-02
A production model Evangelion built from Units 00 and 01 data, the first meant for real combat. Designed in Japan; final assembly and activation tests were in Germany. Asuka calls it "the real Evangelion." Its full power showed in the movie's battle with the nine Mass Produced Evas, but...
#344 EVA-03
The production model Evangelion after Unit-02, piloted by Toji Suzuhara, the Fourth Child. Shipped to Japan from NERV's 1st Branch in America, it was taken over by the 13th Angel Bardiel, with Toji aboard, in its activation test at the Matsushiro 2nd Test Site.
It is written "3" not "San" in Japanese because it was built in America.
#345 3rd Angel Sachiel
The third Angel identified. The first to appear in 15 years since Second Impact in Antarctica, and the first Angel Unit-01 fought. In the TV series an N2 mine badly damaged it, but it regenerated into a second version with two faces.
#346 4th Angel Shamshel
The fourth Angel identified. It appeared three weeks after Sachiel and fought Unit-01. Its core was destroyed and it stopped, but its body stayed almost intact, and NERV took it as a sample.
#347 5th Angel Ramiel
The fifth Angel identified. Shaped like an octahedron, it fires a powerful particle beam at anyone who nears. To beat it, NERV used the Eva positron rifle, drawing power from all of Japan.
#348 6th Angel Gaghiel
The sixth Angel identified. An aquatic Angel that attacked the U.N. fleet carrying Unit-02. Over 500 m long, yet agile in the water.
#349 7th Angel Israfel
The seventh Angel identified.
Cut in half by Unit-02, it split into two bodies, A and B (as a many-celled organism divides into many), and beat Units 01 and 02.
#350 Israfel (A)
One half of the split 7th Angel Israfel. Halted by the U.N. forces' attack, it healed itself and attacked Tokyo-3 again. To strike the cores of A and B at once (the unison attack), Shinji and Asuka trained with a dance.
#351 Israfel (B)
One half of the split 7th Angel Israfel. Halted by the U.N. forces' attack, it healed itself and attacked Tokyo-3 again. It can also merge back into one.
#352 9th Angel Matarael
The ninth Angel identified. When it came, someone had cut NERV HQ's power, shutting it down. The battle was also the first to send out all three Evas at once. The eye-like pattern on its underside oozes acid that melted NERV HQ's special armor, but its defense seemed weak; pallet rifle fire at its core killed it.
#353 13th Angel Bardiel
The thirteenth Angel identified. Hidden in a thunderhead, it touched EVA-03 en route to the Matsushiro Test Site. Just after the activation test, it took over Unit-03 with Toji aboard, and was declared an Angel. It beat Units 00 and 02, but Unit-01 on the Dummy System destroyed it.
#354 14th Angel Zeruel
The fourteenth Angel identified. It broke into the GeoFront and, stronger than any Angel before, beat Units 02 and 00. It then fought the berserk Unit-01 and was devoured.
#355 Kaworu Nagisa
The Fifth Child, sent by SEELE. Like Rei Ayanami, his past is a total mystery. He can change his sync ratio at will, astonishing the NERV staff. He reaches out to Shinji, whose heart opens to his honesty and kindness. But he is Tabris, the 17th Angel sent by SEELE, and he invades Terminal Dogma with the unmanned Unit-02. He died by his own wish, crushed in the hand of Shinji's Unit-01.
Famously, joining the kanji "shi" and "sha" of episode 24's title, "The Final Messenger," makes his surname, Nagisa.
#356 Mass Production EVA
Eva series units sent to NERV HQ by SEELE, the secret group running the Human Instrumentality Project, to stop Gendo from pursuing it off their schedule. Nine exist. Unlike earlier Evas, they come standard with flight and S2 Engines. They are run by Dummy Plugs, which seem to hold Kaworu Nagisa's data.
#357 Mass Production EVA
Flight mode, with wings from its back. Their circling descent on NERV HQ recalls a certain ninja team.
#358 Macross
A space battleship rebuilt over 10 years by the U.N. Government and U.N. Forces, who learned of interstellar war and aliens from an alien ship that fell on South Ataria Island in 1999. First called ASS-1 (Alien Star Ship), it was then named SDF-1 (SUPER DIMENSION FORTRESS) Macross.
A huge ship over 1 km long, it docks with the VF-1 Valkyrie carriers Armd-1 and Armd-2 (in the TV series, the attack carrier Daedalus and assault ship Prometheus). But it is a mass of Overtechnology, with many unknown areas (sealed blocks) even after the refit.
In the TV series the Macross was a booby trap sent to Earth by the Supervision Army against the Zentradi; in the movie it seems to have been a mid-size Meltrandi gunship.
In the TV series, after its first fold failed at South Ataria, it took in the island's buildings and made a city inside; in the movie it was also meant as an emigrant ship, so the city seems to have been there from the start.
#359 Macross
The Macross after transforming, able to fire its main gun. On its first fold (warp), the energy pipes linking engine and main gun vanished, so it rearranged its hull blocks into this form to reconnect them. This is called the Trans-Formation. This form is an irregular one; its main reaction engines, now its legs, cannot drive it forward, so it is slower. It also affects the city inside, so citizens are warned before it transforms.
But besides the main gun, it can use pinpoint barriers for melee unfit for a battleship, so its overall power rises. The game's "Armd Attack" is the TV series' "Daedalus Attack."

The air combat/cruise form of the VF-1A Valkyrie, a transformable anti-alien fighter the U.N. Forces built with Macross Overtechnology. Called Fighter or F mode. Its reaction engines need no propellant, for nearly unlimited range. Main arms: the belly gun pod and the laser gun that is its head in Battroid form. Hikaru's colors in the movie.
