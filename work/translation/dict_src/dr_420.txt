#420 Giant Robo
A giant robot the BF Group had Dr. Kusama build, one of three robots of the GR Project, for land combat. It runs not on the clean, endlessly reusable Shizuma Drive but on a nuclear reactor. So it could move even amid the Bashtarle Phenomenon (Shizuma Drive shutdown) caused by the BF Group's giant sphere Vogler. But the reactor's danger once got it sealed away.
It acts on orders from the wristwatch controller of Dr. Kusama's son Daisaku, but decides details itself. Keyed to Daisaku's voiceprint, it obeys no one else. When Daisaku is in danger, its auto-guard circuit activates it, putting his life first.
In fact its right leg has an emergency shelter where one can live for several days.
#421 Ginrei Robo Type-1
A female robot Ginrei piloted in the side story "Tetsuwan GinRei," modeled on Ginrei herself.
#422 Ginrei Robo Type-2
A female robot Ginrei piloted in the side story "Tetsuwan GinRei." Ginrei Robo Type-1 transforms into this form, which is said to be far stronger, faster and more beautiful. It is actually the same unit as Type-1, which becomes this type by transforming.
#423 Ishinryu Akatsuki
A BF Group monster robot that turns into a train, carrying helicopters for its agents. It fought Giant Robo in Nanjing.
#424 Uranus
A BF Group monster robot shaped like a dogu clay figure.
Ivan's car turns into its head cockpit. A special barrier gives it strong defense. Beaten by Giant Robo, it was rebuilt for a rematch in Shanghai.
#425 GR2
A giant robot the BF Group had Dr. Kusama build, one of three robots of the GR Project, for underwater combat. In the anime it blocks Daisaku and Giant Robo as they flee a BF base. It seems voice-controlled like the Robo; Cervantes stood on its horn to give it orders. Fast underwater, it toyed with Daisaku's Robo, until the Robo seized its horn and blasted it with a rocket bazooka point-blank. GR3 is for air combat, seen in one shot of the opening.
#426 Shockwave Alberto
One of the BF Magnificent Ten, who controls shockwaves as his name suggests. A proud man who longs to slay Taiso himself, for killing his sworn friend Dazzling Cervantes in the Domino Operation and taking his right eye.
But their duel ends unsettled, and his anger and emptiness change him; distrusting the strategist Komei, he later leaves the BF Group.
#427 SRX
Formally the SUPER ROBOT X-TYPE. The ultimate all-purpose, one-hit-kill Personal Trooper of the SRX Project, built with all the know-how of the G Series, H Series, Valsion and more. It consists of the R-1, R-2 Powered and R-3 Powered; main pilot is Ens. Ryusei Date of the SRX Team. Its concept: "a humanoid weapon with the traits of both super and real robots, able to turn the tide as one unit or as three." So its firepower is unrivaled, used to destroy enemy bases and wipe out units fast. Balancing its high-output Tronium Engine, fueled by the alien resource tronium, against frame durability, it can combine only about 10 times before an overhaul. A Tronium Engine at full drive that explodes turns everything within 50 km to ash; the SRX is a matchless but double-edged sword.
The SRX flies using the R-3 Powered's T-LINK Flight Unit. In space, it can even fight without the R-3 Powered's Plus Parts, which form its legs.
The SRX, R-1, R-3 and R-GUN carry the "Uranus System," an aid to the T-LINK System. It monitors the pilot and damage, and in a dire pinch it switches on automatically to keep the pilot alive. It draws the pilot's telekinesis to the limit and the unit gains over twice its power, but this greatly risks the pilot's mental collapse. In short, activating the Uranus System means the SRX going berserk. This SRX is only a prototype; production units are to follow. Its finisher, the Unstoppable Supreme Sword, is formally the Zol Orichalconium Sword (Z.O. Sword), and the Unstoppable TK Burst Slash the Hi-Zol Orichalconium Sword.
#428 R-1
Formally the REAL PERSONAL TROOPER TYPE-1.
The first R Series Personal Trooper of the SRX Project, piloted by Ens. Ryusei Date of the SRX Team. Made for close combat and melee, it carries solid-ammo weapons to reliably damage foes. Its forerunners were the PTX-06 Wildraptor, the first transformable PT (wrecked in testing), and the RTX-009 Huckebein (fusion type), and it inherits the strengths of both. The R-1 was also built as the ultimate standard PT, the line of the PTX-01 Gespenst and PTX-05 Wildschwein, using the latest PT and EOT tech throughout. But for stability it runs on a fusion engine, not a Tronium Engine. Its frame became the base of later PTs, so weapons swap easily with successors and new arms are easy to add. It also has a prototype T-LINK System (telekinesis sensor/amplifier) and can use the T-Link Knuckle, a T-LINK Field weapon. Its armor is Zol Orichalconium, a strengthened, refined, flexible form of orichalconium from La Gias (without magical protection). It can transform into its flight form, the R-Wing.
#429 R-Wing
The R-1's flight form (flyer mode). It cannot fight in melee, the R-1's strength, so it is used mainly for travel. Its nose is the shield the R-1 uses in PT form.
#430 R-2
Formally the REAL PERSONAL TROOPER TYPE-2.
The second R Series Personal Trooper of the SRX Project, piloted by Ens. Raidiese F. Branstein of the SRX Team. A heavy artillery PT, mainly armed with strong beam weapons. Its Tronium Engine, also the SRX's power source, gives it high firepower and thick armor. But the engine's output is unstable, and its weight makes it slow. Thermonuclear jets in its legs let it hover, so it moves better than it looks. Its role is to back the melee R-1 and the ranged R-3, but in practice it often pairs with the R-1 up front in place of the R-3, the key to the T-LINK System. It was also meant to have a T-LINK System, but with too few telekinetics, it was built as a normal PT. Its right arm has a wired beam chakram, its left a beam shield. The chakram was first a telekinetic weapon, but became wired when the concept changed. It is also the prototype of the Huckebein Mk-II's Chakram Shooter.
#431 R-2 Powered
The R-2 with its Plus Parts: the shoulder Hi-Zol Launcher (a heavy particle cannon) and a solid shield.
#432 R-3
Formally the REAL PERSONAL TROOPER TYPE-3.
The third R Series Personal Trooper of the SRX Project, piloted by Capt. Aya Kobayashi of the SRX Team. A light PT for command and long range, known for remote telekinetic weapons using the T-LINK System and strong targeting and intel. Light and fast, but its armor is the thinnest of the three. It is fully built around the T-LINK System (telekinesis sensor/amplifier), so only telekinetics can pilot it. Long links also strain the pilot (past the limit, the T-LINK breaker trips). The R-3's T-LINK System is the SRX's main system, so damage to it before combining is fatal to the SRX. It was meant to fight in tandem with the R-1, but for those reasons it stays back in battle, commanding and scouting. Its cockpit is not a seat but a capsule holding the pilot upright. It looks female because it was modeled on the Valsione, a Super Machinery Humanoid built by the DC.
#433 R-3 Powered
The R-3 with its Plus Parts, covering its thin armor; the T-LINK Flight System lets it fly.
#434 R-GUN Powered
The SRX Project's PT R-GUN with its Plus Parts. No. 1 of the RW Series of SRX option weapons, it turns from PT form into the gun-shaped HTB (Hyper Tronium Buster) Cannon. First built as a high-output heavy metal particle cannon for the SRX, it was given a PT mode so it could move and fight alone, easing transport and handling. Like the R-1 and R-3 it has a T-LINK System, letting the pilot's telekinesis control the beam's focus and aim. It also has the T-LINK Field, a telekinetic barrier, and flies with the T-LINK Flight System in its Plus Parts. Its new Tronium Engine outputs more than the R-2's, outclassing the other three in power and firepower. So it is the highest-spec R Series unit yet, but its output is so unstable it must normally be run held back.
#435 Zfylud
The strongest mobile weapon of the Ze Balmary Empire's Galactic Frontier Inspection Army (the Aerogaters). The personal unit of 7th Fleet commander Laodicea Judecca Gozzo, built for wars with unknown civilizations.
It normally forms part of the core system of the Inspection flagship Hermodr. Its body is Zfylud Crystal, holding autonomous, self-aware metal cells, and a crystal forms its core. The core links to Megillot scouts fitted with Zfylud Crystal, which send it data on the weapons of hostile civilizations. Zfylud analyzes that data and makes it its own. In short, the stronger the civilization it faces, the stronger it grows. But gathering, analysis and evolution take much time and energy, so it cannot sortie on short notice (hence the Judecca was built). Its shape also changes with the data of enemy weapons, so it has no fixed form. Its self-regeneration far outstrips other units; if even one shard of Zfylud Crystal remains, it regrows its whole body (pilot included) in no time.
Like the Judecca and Astranagant, it runs on a quantum wave engine. The Galactic Frontier Inspection Army has one per fleet flagship, so seven Zfyluds exist now (the many Zfyluds in the final stage are copies made by lifting the crystal's limiter).
It is named after Ze Balmary's creator god. Earth's code name for it: Nightmare Crystal.
#436 Judecca
A giant mobile weapon of the Ze Balmary Empire's 7th Frontier Fleet (the Aerogaters). Built specially by Euzeth Gozzo for his own ends; only two exist. So among the Inspection Army, only the 7th Frontier Fleet has them. It normally sits in the flagship Hermodr, controlling mass-produced Megillots.
It runs on a quantum wave engine, and its body is Zfylud Crystal, autonomous, self-aware metal cells.
The Judecca and its pilot are linked by a Calcelia Pulse transmitter; Levi Tolar in particular seems mind-controlled by it. The system is very like the T-LINK System, and the Judecca can use a barrier much like a T-LINK Field. Its self-regenerating Zfylud Crystal also lets it repair damage. Earth's code name: White Death Cross.
#437 Judecca
A giant mobile weapon of the Ze Balmary Empire's 7th Frontier Fleet, Euzeth Gozzo's personal unit. It matches Levi's unit in basic specs, but uniquely has the Crossgate Paradigm System (limited causality control).
But the system is unfinished, not yet able to bend causality at will or cross time and space. Once it is complete, this Judecca should become the strongest weapon, surpassing even Zfylud. Used with the quantum wave engine, the system can also shift phase at will, keeping the same being across parallel universes.
Earth's code name: Black Death Cross.
#438 Hermodr
Flagship of the Ze Balmary Empire's 7th Frontier Inspection Fleet. Earth's code name: Green Flower. It is also an interstellar emigrant ship, with several cities and plants inside. It has a warp drive called the Crossgate Drive, and its main gun, the Legion Buster, is said to destroy several colonies in one shot.
#439 Megillot
A mass-produced unmanned Aerogater weapon. Earth's code name: Bugs. It contains a crystal of autonomous metal cells that can regenerate and multiply. Fast and versatile, it works in any setting. Small, yet a match for a Personal Trooper, and its high-performance sensors make it a scout too. Data it gathers goes to the Aerogater flagship. The most numerous Aerogater weapon; they usually invade and subdue planets with Megillots alone.
#440 Zechariah
An Aerogater humanoid mobile weapon, their main manned mass-produced unit, good at close range. Not very powerful, but versatile and agile, it is sent in against civilizations the Megillots cannot handle. In use for 200 years; the current Zechariah is the 18th, much-improved model. Earth's code name: Soldier.
#441 Habbakuk
A heavy Aerogater mobile weapon. Earth's code name: Fatman. Built for long-range fire in fleet and fortress battles, it stresses power and defense, but is a slow mobile gun platform. It usually works with the close-range Zechariah.
#442 Ezekiel
An Aerogater humanoid mobile weapon, a higher model than the Zechariah. Earth's code name: Knight. It has the traits of both the Zechariah and Habbakuk and outperforms both. Too costly for mass production, it serves as a squad leader or guard unit.
#443 Antenora
A heavy Aerogater mobile weapon. Earth's code name: Mono Leg. Built by Euzeth Gozzo as a prototype of the Judecca, so only the 7th Frontier Fleet has it. It usually runs on an autonomous circuit, but commanders sometimes pilot it. Made of self-healing Zfylud Crystal, with a prototype quantum wave engine, it is a cut above other mass-produced models.
#444 Astranagant
A jet-black humanoid mobile weapon Ingram built for himself after returning to the 7th Frontier Fleet (the Aerogaters). Earth's code name: Black Angel. The ultimate unit, merging tech Ingram gained on Earth, La Gias tech like the Masou Kishin, and Aerogater tech. It runs mainly on a quantum wave engine, with weapons modeled on SRX Project units and the Masou Kishin (Z.O. Sword = Zol Orichalconium Sword, Gun Familiar and more). Its Tipler Cylinder can draw energy from other dimensional layers and parallel universes, and is said to allow travel through time and space. It also has a T-LINK System, controlling the unit and weapons by telekinesis and using a T-LINK Field. Its Zfylud Crystal armor repairs itself when damaged. For now, it is the only unit that can match Shu Shirakawa's Granzon.
#445 Cybuster
The Masou Kishin designed and built by the alchemist Wendy Lasm Iknart in the Holy Kingdom of Langran's Masouki Project. Blessed by the wind spirit Cyfis, it is called the "Masou Kishin of Wind."
Its main power is a Fulcanelli perpetual engine, which needs the pilot's prana to run efficiently. It uses ether (massless energy at rest in absolute space) as a magic medium for thrust.
Its orichalconium armor has talismanic numerological patterns sealed in at the particle level, for strong magical defense. Its main computer is a Laplace Demon type; with enough magic from the pilot, it can calculate all causality and predict any event. "Cybuster" is not its true name but a guardian name to ward off magical attacks. Chosen as its pilot, Masaki went to the surface and met a Federation scout, which took it for an Aerogater and tagged it AGX-05; the Far East Branch logged it as a UFO.
#446 Cybird
Cybuster's cruise form, modeled on Dishnas, the sacred bird of the Holy Kingdom of Langran. Cybuster is already fast, but this form flies even faster. The transform call: "Cybuster, change, Cybird!"
#447 Granzon
An anti-alien Armored Module built by the Divine Crusaders (DC). The third Valsion series unit, but so different in form and concept that it got the code DCAM-00.
Its designer and pilot is Shu Shirakawa, head of the DC Japan branch. It runs on an annihilation engine Shu built from the Aerogaters' black hole engine. A Kabbalah Program he secretly put in its drive code also lets it use astral energy. It has a T-LINK System too, allowing remote control by Shu's thought waves. Its armor is super-resistant titanium, strengthened at the particle level. Built for firepower, armor and defense over speed, this super high-performance unit is said to have used two-thirds of the DC's budget. Its know-how passed to the SRX Project's R Series, so it also bears the code "R-0."
Its Degeneration Cannon is a prototype, usable without its true form, "Neo Granzon." This weapon is later upgraded into Neo Granzon's strongest weapon.
#448 Valsione R
A rebuild of the Valsione, the second Super Machinery Humanoid built by the DC. The first is the Valsion, called the ultimate robot, mankind's first humanoid weapon with EOT. Its creator Dr. Bian Zoldark named his daughter Lune its pilot, but she refused because she hated its design. So the Valsione became a female unit with a concept unlike the Valsion's (the doctor himself wanted a unit that fixed the Valsion's flaws: all firepower, low mobility).
The Jupitorians then rebuilt it (partly to study Earth's EOT weapons), adding Aerogater tech to make the "Valsione R." Its power source became a prototype quantum wave engine, boosting overall performance over the Valsione. According to Lune, the R stands for "RETURN" or "RETURNS."
