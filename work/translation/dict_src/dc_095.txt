#95 Amuro Ray | Amuro | Toru Furuya
Leader of Londo Bell's MS team. Rank: captain. In the One Year War he flew the RX-78 Gundam, setting records and proving to the Federation that Newtypes exist. Fearing his power, the Federation later kept him under house arrest. He later joined Karaba, the AEUG's support group, leading its MS team. After the First Neo Zeon War he joined Londo Bell, the Federation's independent task force, fighting Neo Zeon under his rival Char Aznable, who plotted to freeze the Earth.
In the One Year War he was a high-strung boy, both naive and spoiled, but the people he met awoke him as a Newtype, and his many trials made him grow greatly. After stopping Axis from falling to Earth in the Second Neo Zeon War, he vanished with Char and the Nu Gundam he helped design.
#96 Bright Noa | Bright | Hirotaka Suzuoki
Captain of the White Base in the One Year War, the Argama in the AEUG, and the flagship Ra Cailum in Londo Bell. Long combat experience makes him decisive.
#97 Kayra Su | Kayra | Shinobu Adachi
A Londo Bell pilot who serves as Amuro's second. As frank as her boyish looks suggest, she was close to chief mechanic Astonaige. Taken hostage by Gyunei in battle, she died before Amuro's eyes.
#98 Chan Agi | Chan
A Federation technical officer who tunes the Nu Gundam. She has feelings for her superior Amuro and throws herself into tuning the psycoframe to help him. During the battle for Axis, she flies a damaged Re-GZ into battle to bring Amuro a psycoframe sample, hoping it will help even a little.
She cuts in between Hathaway and Quess and shoots down the Alpha Azieru, but is shot by Hathaway, enraged at Quess's death.
#99 Quess Paraya | Quess | Maria Kawamura
Daughter of Adenauer Paraya, Federation vice-minister of staff. Resenting her father and his mistress, she finds a place with Amuro on the Ra Cailum. But jealous of Chan, she leaves for Char. Starved for love, she gives her heart to Char, who needs her, and fights Londo Bell in the Alpha Azieru. She dies shielding Hathaway, who followed her into battle.
#100 Gyunei Guss | Gyunei | Koichi Yamadera
A Neo Zeon Cyber Newtype from Nanai's Newtype lab who flies the Jagd Doga. He lost his parents to the colony drop in the One Year War, and dislikes some of Char's methods. More stable than past Cyber Newtypes, he is proud of his skills and hates being called one. Drawn to Quess, whom he sees as a true Newtype, he stays near her to touch her power. He secretly sees Char as a rival and is obsessed with beating Amuro, but Amuro shoots him down.
#101 Rezin Schnyder | Rezin | Kazue Ikura
A Neo Zeon pilot.
She flies a tuned blue Geara Doga. A veteran who despises Cyber Newtypes and denies such power.
But she is shot down by the machine guns of Chan, whose Newtype power briefly woke through the psycoframe, and dies unable to believe it.
#102 Nanai Miguel | Nanai | Yoshiko Sakakibara
Head of Neo Zeon's Newtype lab and a tactical officer. She is also Char's lover, and rumor says that is how she got her post, but she is a truly able leader, and takes overall command once Char sorties.
Privately, too, she understood Char and tried to be his support.
#103 Seabook Arno | Seabook | Koji Tsujitani
A civilian boy in the engineering course of a high school in Frontier IV. When the Crossbone Vanguard invades, he sees Dorel take away his childhood friend Cecily. Fleeing the fighting with his friends, he escapes the colony to the Federation training ship Space Ark. There he fights the Crossbone Vanguard as pilot of the new mobile suit F91. He has Newtype potential and is highly compatible with the bio-computer his mother Monica developed. A kind boy who never forgets to protect his friends, even in fierce battle.
#104 Cecily Fairchild | Cecily | Yumi Toma
A girl at Seabook's high school. She is actually the eldest daughter of the Ronah family, leaders of Cosmo Aristocracy, and her real name is Berah Ronah. The Vanguard's invasion brings her home. Her grandfather Meitzer wants her as its symbol, and she tries to obey, but when she meets Seabook again, he persuades her to cast off the name Berah Ronah by choice.
#105 Birgit Pirjo | Birgit | Yoku Shioya
A Federation MS pilot. Really only a cadet, he is still a rookie. He can be sarcastic, needling Seabook, who is rumored to be a Newtype, but he looks after Seabook and Cecily, who have less combat experience. He fought hard against the Bugs that invaded Frontier I, but was killed.
#106 Carozzo Ronah | Carozzo | Masaaki Maeda
Supreme commander of the Crossbone Vanguard, also called the Iron Mask. Ashamed of his weakness when his wife Nadia left, he took the mask and enhanced his body for Meitzer Ronah's ideals. He can survive in space unaided, pry open MS armor bare-handed and control machines with brain waves. He coldly pursues the Rafflesia Project to wipe out most of mankind. He fights his daughter Cecily and Seabook in the giant mobile armor Rafflesia, and is destroyed by the F91.
#107 Dorel Ronah | Dorel | Takeshi Kusao
A field commander of the Crossbone Vanguard. Carozzo's son and Cecily's half-brother. Ordered to bring her back to the Crossbone, he led the attack on Frontier IV. He earned command of an MS battalion by his own skill, not his father's name.
#108 Zabine Chareux | Zabine | Kiyoyuki Yanada
A young ace who leads the Black Vanguard, the Crossbone's elite unit. As Berah Ronah's guardian, he taught her to pilot, and her quick learning made him believe in Newtypes. Devoted to Cosmo Aristocracy, he believes in removing those who cannot control their emotions. But he questions the Iron Mask, and is angered by the Bugs, which betray his ideals. He killed Carozzo's right hand, Gillet Krueger, and let the battered F91 go after it defeated Carozzo.
#109 Gillet Krueger | Gillet | Kiyoshi Kobayashi
A Crossbone Vanguard battle commander and Carozzo's right-hand man. On his orders he secretly advances the Rafflesia Project to purge humanity. Zabine learns of it and shoots him dead in anger.
#110 Uso Ewin | Uso | Daisuke Sakaguchi
A civilian boy raised in Kasarelia on Earth. Caught in the war when Zanscare's BESPA invades Europe, he ends up helping the League Militaire. His parents' training and a simulator left at home made him skilled at survival and MS piloting. He becomes the pilot-starved League's ace. Called "special," he carries the adults' hopes, like it or not. He mainly flies the Victory and V2 Gundams. Through the deaths of friends, battles with Katejina, the girl he admired who joined Zanscare, reunions with his parents and his friend Shakti being drawn in, he grows with a positive will.
Out of the cockpit he is a normal, lively and competitive boy, doted on by the Shrike Team and other older women. After the war he returns to Kasarelia with Shakti and his friends and resumes his old life.
#111 Odelo Henrik | Odelo | Masayuki Nakata
Leader of the children who lost their homes to BESPA attacks and took refuge with the League Militaire. A joker, but a kind boy with a strong sense of justice who calms a hotheaded Uso and acts as big brother to little Suzy. Competing with Uso, he later pilots the Gun Blaster. He falls for Elisha, a new friend from Highland, and wins her heart. But in the final battle at the Angel Halo as it descends, Katejina shoots him down.
#112 Marbet Fingerhat | Marbet | Fumiko Shiraishi
A League Militaire pilot. She was the Victory Gundam's test pilot in Uhig, but gave its cockpit to Uso after being wounded. Like a big sister to Uso, she teaches him a warrior's mindset and supports him. She is Oliver's lover and sometimes jealous of the Shrike Team, but they hold a wedding, if only as a cover, and she later carries his child. After the war she lives in Kasarelia with Uso and the others.
#113 Oliver Inoe | Oliver | Keiichi Sonobe
Leader of the Shrike Team, the League Militaire's MS unit. He joins Uso's group on their way to the mass driver to reach space. Also the Shrike Team's instructor, he is a fine role model for Uso as a warrior. He must mediate between Marbet and Junko, but holds a wedding with his lover Marbet, if only as a cover. He dies ramming the Motorad fleet in the V2's Core Fighter to stop its advance, leaving a child with Marbet.
#114 Junko Jenko | Junko | Yuko Kobayashi
A member of the Shrike Team, the League Militaire's MS unit. A leader to the others, they call her "Sis." She dotes on Uso, and sometimes smiles wryly at his earnest, blunt way of talking. She seems to have had feelings for Oliver, which may be why she often clashed with Marbet. She died in the fight over the Big Cannon at the space fortress Kaiilas Guilly.
#115 Mahalia Merrill | Mahalia | Mari Maruta
A member of the Shrike Team. Like the others she dotes on Uso, and seeing her late brother in him, tells him her story. She sorties with lipstick on her cheeks to avenge Helen, but dies protecting Uso.
#116 Kate Bush | Kate | Shinobu Adachi
A member of the Shrike Team. In the battle for the mass driver, she holds up the catapult in place of a pillar broken by BESPA, but is attacked while unable to move, and dies.
#117 Helen Jackson | Helen | Rika Fukami
A member of the Shrike Team. Feisty, she downs an enemy to protect a friendly transport, but is caught off guard after, becoming the first Shrike to die.
#118 Peggy Lee | Peggy | Kumiko Watanabe
A member of the Shrike Team, said to be second only to Junko in skill. Though wounded, she sorties and infiltrates Zanscare with Uso, but dies shielding Uso from Chronicle.
#119 Connie Francis | Connie | Satomi Korogi
A member of the Shrike Team. The last survivor of its original members, she protects Uso and fights as a League Militaire mainstay with the new members. In the final battle, Katejina shoots her down as she moves to help Uso against Chronicle.
#120 Juca Meilasch | Juca | Atsuko Tanaka
A survivor of the Federation's Bagre Team, she joins the Shrike Team, believing in the League Militaire. A skilled pilot, but every unit she served in was wiped out, earning her the name "Reaper." Feisty yet calm, she dies in a suicide charge to let Uso reach the Angel Halo.
#121 Francesca O'Hara | Francesca | Hiromi Ishikawa
A new Shrike Team member assigned after many original members died. She is best friends with Miliera, who joined with her. She sorties with the V2's Mega Beam Shield, but finds its power too much and hands it to Uso. In the final battle at the descending Angel Halo, Katejina shoots her down.
#122 Miliera Katan | Miliera | Narumi Hidaka
A new Shrike Team member assigned after many original members died. She is best friends with Francesca, who joined with her. Enraged when Katejina shoots down Francesca, she attacks her, but is killed in turn.
#123 Robert Gomez | Gomez | Osamu Kato
A Federation captain ordered to transport League Militaire units on Earth. He reaches space in the old battleship Reinforce and fights on as captain of the Reinforce Jr. At first a complainer, he grows responsible, and in the final battle at the Angel Halo he rams the Zugan fleet with the Reinforce Jr. and the League's old men.
#124 Shakti Kareen | Shakti
Uso's childhood friend. She waited alone in Kasarelia for her missing mother to return. Her mother is Queen Maria of Zanscare, and Shakti herself has great talent as a psychicker. Quiet and modest, but strong at heart, she speaks her mind when needed. Worried for Uso the pilot, she stays with him, but a chance reunion with her mother drags her into the war. In the final battle she goes to the Angel Halo herself to save the psychickers, activating it to stop its fall. After the war she returns to Kasarelia with Uso and her friends and resumes her old life.
#125 Haro | Haro | Rica Matsumoto
Uso's beloved AI module. A pet robot loved for over 40 years, but far more autonomous than the model Amuro built. It can project images on a balloon and hold simple conversations. It rides in the V2 with Uso as his navigator.
#126 Warren Trace | Warren
One of the children who lost their homes to BESPA and took refuge with the League Militaire. A bit timid, he grows more active through the fighting. He falls for Martina, a new friend from Highland, but unlike Odelo, never wins her over.
He can give himself a fever at will, and once used it to get past a checkpoint. After the war he lives in Kasarelia with Uso and the others.
#127 Suzy Relane | Suzy
One of the children who lost their homes to BESPA and took refuge with the League Militaire. Bright and lively, she is the League's mascot along with Karlmann and Shakti's dog Flanders. She cried as she comforted Uso when he lost his mother, a support to everyone. After the war she lives in Kasarelia with Uso and the others.
