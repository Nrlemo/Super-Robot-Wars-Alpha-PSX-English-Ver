A pilot of the Valkyrie Skull Squadron.

Invited by Roy Focker, once his father's student, he enlisted, and grows as a person through the war with the Zentradi.
Modest and indecisive, yet he can get hotheaded and break orders. His heart wavers between two women, Lynn Minmay and Misa Hayase.

#303 Kim Kabirov | Kim
A Macross bridge operator, one of the "Bridge Bunnies."

#310 Milia Fallyna Jenius | Milia | Eri Takeda
Milia after being micronized to infiltrate the Macross.

#318 Tetsugyu | Tetsugyu
An IPO Beijing Expert, code-named "the Black Whirlwind" for his Ki Whirlwind. He loves Ginrei, who ignores him, and sees Taiso as a big brother.

The BF Group's top strategist, author of every plan, who holds the group together. Commanding the "Three Guardian Units" that protect Big Fire, the true boss of the BF Group, he gives orders to the Ten in Big Fire's name. Calm and aloof, as if he sees through all, his true intent is a mystery.
#330 Ryusei Date | Ryusei | Shinichiro Miki

For Rai's past, see the soundtrack "SRW Alpha Original Score 2: Chapter of the Earth," on sale now.
#332 Aya Kobayashi | Aya | Yumi Toma

A key member of the SRX Project and creator of the T-LINK System. Also Aya's father. #335 Kirk Hamill | Kirk

#340 Levi Tolar | Levi | Ai Orikasa

#341 Levi Tolar | Levi | Ai Orikasa

Shu Shirakawa's familiar. Unlike the taciturn Shu, it is a chatterbox and greedy for money. It is modeled after the loshen, a bird of Langran. It seems to be forbidden to talk while Shu is on the surface.
