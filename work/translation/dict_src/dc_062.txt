#62 Ben Wooder | Ben | Ryunosuke Obayashi
Buran's second-in-command, who leads the Thourien after his death. He stops at nothing, attacking Hong Kong to bring down the Audhumla and taking Mirai and her children hostage. Yet many men volunteered for his ramming attack on the Audhumla, showing their trust. Amuro foiled the attack, and Ben went down with the Thourien.
#63 Rosamia Badam | Rosamia | Kayoko Fujii
A Cyber Newtype conditioned at the Augusta Newtype Lab. Her trauma from the One Year War colony drop was used to make her hate the AEUG. Katz and Kamille downed her Gaplant as she attacked the Audhumla. Made to see Kamille as her brother, she met him in Side 2, controlled by Gates. She finally attacked the Argama in the Psyco Gundam Mk-II, her mind by then near collapse from repeated conditioning.
#64 Gates Capa | Gates | Kazuki Yao
A Cyber Newtype conditioned at the Augusta Newtype Lab. He plays the unstable Rosamia's brother to keep watch on her. His Baund Doc was linked to his mind, so her death hurt him deeply.
#65 Yazan Gable | Yazan | Hochu Otsuka
A Titans pilot of the first rank who handles agile machines like the Gaplant and Hambrabi with ease. Also a schemer, he had Jamaican of the Alexandria killed. Drawn by Scirocco's grand vision, he later aided his ambitions. With his men Ramsus and Dunkel he tormented Quattro and Kamille, but was shot down by Kamille, whose rage at his cruelty awakened the Zeta's bio-sensor.
In episode 1 of Gundam ZZ he reaches Shangri-La in Side 1 and tries to steal MS from the docked Argama, again angering Judau with his cruelty. Teaming up with the junk dealer Gemon Bajack, he attacks the Argama in a Gaze, but is finally shot down by Judau's Zeta.
#66 Dunkel Cooper | Dunkel | Masami Kikuchi
A Titans member under Yazan. Flying a Hambrabi with Ramsus, their three-unit teamwork troubled Quattro and Kamille. He was shot down by Kamille's Zeta.
#67 Ramsus Hasa | Ramsus | Shingo Hiromori
A Titans pilot directly under Yazan. Flying a Hambrabi with Yazan and Dunkel, their three-unit teamwork troubled Quattro and Kamille. He was shot down by Emma's Super Gundam.
#68 Paptimus Scirocco | Scirocco | Bin Shimada
A man who returned from Jupiter on the transport Jupitris. A shrewd politician and cunning schemer, he removes obstacles like Jamitov and takes over the Titans. A powerful Newtype and superb pilot, he matches Kamille and Haman in mobile suits of his own design. He claims to seek humanity's evolution, but is full of self-righteous arrogance, wanting to rule all, and treats the war like a game he watches from above.
His unique charisma drew Sarah, Reccoa and Yazan to him. In the final battle he loses to Kamille's Zeta, which rejects his arrogance, but breaks Kamille's mind as he dies.
#69 Sarah Zabiarov | Sarah | Yuko Mizutani
A Titans member directly under Scirocco. She follows him for seeing her Newtype potential, though Kamille and Katz warn her of the danger. Unable to leave the man who needs her, she dies shielding Scirocco from Katz. In the final battle her spirit tries to protect him, but Katz and the other spirits persuade her to leave all to Kamille.
#70 Reccoa Londe | Reccoa | Masako Katsuki
An AEUG member mainly in charge of intelligence. Like Emma, she is a big sister to Kamille, and was close to Quattro. Captured by the Titans while scouting before the Jaburo raid, she became a pilot after her rescue. Doubting a life of constant fighting, she met Scirocco, found her place in being needed by him, and left the AEUG.
She faced Kamille and Emma, who could not understand her, and fell in near mutual destruction with Emma's Mk-II.
#71 Jamitov Hymen | Jamitov
Founder and leader of the Titans. A cold schemer who formed the Titans on the pretext of hunting Zeon remnants to seize the Earth Sphere. He was killed by Scirocco, who took over the Titans. He appears in Gundam 0083 as well, dealing with Cima Garahau.
#72 Haman Karn | Haman | Yoshiko Sakakibara
Regent to Mineva Zabi, ruling Axis. Her father, Maharaja Karn, led the Zeon fleet that fled the Earth Sphere. After his death she united Axis with her charisma. Seeking to rule the Earth Sphere, she brought Axis back and made contact with both the AEUG and the Titans. But her history with Quattro led to a split with the AEUG, and she aimed to reap the spoils after their final battle. In Gundam ZZ she founds Neo Zeon while the Federation is weak, becoming its real leader. She seizes the Federation assembly in Dakar without bloodshed, but Glemy's rebellion keeps her ambition from coming true. In the end she dies in a duel with Judau's ZZ Gundam.
She believed in changing society through Newtypes, but Quattro, Kamille and Judau rejected her methods. Her twisted ways came from duty and loneliness; at heart she seemed to long for Char, once her emotional anchor.
A superb Newtype and pilot, she flies the Qubeley, symbol of Neo Zeon. Like Char, she rarely wore a normal suit in battle.
#73 Mineva Zabi | Mineva
Daughter of Vice Admiral Dozle Zabi, and the sole rightful Zabi heir. Haman made her the figurehead of Zeon's revival and filled her with twisted ideals. The Mineva on Axis was later revealed to be a double, and the real one's whereabouts remained unknown.
#74 Judau Ashta | Judau | Kazuki Yao
A civilian boy of Shangri-La, a colony in Side 1. He supported himself and his sister Leina as a junk dealer. He tried to steal MS from the docked Argama, but his sense of justice led him to drive off Yazan and Neo Zeon in the Zeta, and he became an Argama pilot.
Energetic and full of drive, he is always cheerful and positive. At first he fought to support Leina, but as friends and Leina were dragged into the war, he came to fight to end it.
A gifted Newtype, he connected with Haman and Ple. After defeating Haman, he joins the Jupiter fleet with Roux to grow further.
#75 Roux Louka | Roux | Naoko Matsui
An AEUG volunteer who brought the ZZ Gundam's Core Fighter to the Argama. Her trademark is a pilot suit with a shooting star. After Judau took the ZZ, she piloted the Zeta. Strong-willed and sure of herself, she clashed with Judau's group, who fought on a whim, but grew to be their friend. Glemy Toto pursued her to her dismay, yet when she shot him at Axis, she wept alone.
After the war she joins the Jupiter fleet with Judau.
#76 Elpeo Ple | Ple | Chieko Honda
A girl conditioned on Axis as a Newtype pilot. She took an instant liking to Judau, and joined the Argama once freed from Glemy's mind control. She loves chocolate parfaits and baths. In daily life she is a small child, often troubling Judau's group with childish whims and jealousy. Yet inside she is lonely, raised only to fight. Blaming herself when Leina went missing, she tried to support Judau, but died fighting Ple Two.
#77 Ple Two | Ple Two | Chieko Honda
Ple's twin sister. Under stronger mind control than Ple, she is more aggressive. She killed Ple on Glemy's orders, but meeting Judau made her question her actions.
In the final battle at Axis in the Quin Mantha, guided by Ple's spirit, she finally leaves Glemy. But her body and mind are at their limit, and after helping Judau escape from his victory over Haman, she dies.
#78 Leina Ashta | Leina | Maya Okamoto
Judau's sister, a kind girl who nags him about his junk business but always worries about him. Aboard the Argama she cooked and did laundry, but by chance fell into Glemy's care and was raised as a lady. When Neo Zeon took Dakar, Haman shot her as she shielded Judau, and she went missing in the fighting. After the war, Sayla Mass reunited her with Judau safe and sound.
#79 Beecha Oleg | Beecha | Shingo Hiromori
One of the Shangri-La junk dealers and leader of Judau's gang. Selfish and fickle, his friends do not trust him much, and he often clashes with Judau.
He once thought of siding with Neo Zeon with Mondo, but returned to the Argama, mostly flying the Hyaku Shiki. After Bright left the front, he captained the Nahel Argama and led his friends.
#80 Iino Abbav | Iino | Masami Kikuchi
One of the Shangri-La junk dealers and Judau's best friend. Good with machines, he served as a pilot, mechanic and bridge crew. Honest and a bit timid, he speaks up when it counts and can act bravely for his friends. In the anime he even dressed as a girl with Judau.
#81 Mondo Agake | Mondo | Kozo Shioya
One of the Shangri-La junk dealers, close to Beecha; the two once tried to leave the Argama. A born mechanic, he repaired an abandoned work MS, the Cattle, in the colony Moon Moon. At first a fence-sitter, he grew as a person after meeting Rasara, Moon Moon's leader.
#82 Elle Vianno | Elle | Eriko Hara
One of the Shangri-La junk dealers, in charge of gathering information. Lively and feisty, she squabbles with Judau but secretly likes him. On the Argama she sees Roux as a rival, and mainly flies the Gundam Mk-II. She shows her girlish side to Judau at times, but in the end returns Beecha's feelings.
#83 Emary Ounce | Emary
Acting captain of Anaheim's supply ship La Vie en Rose. A capable leader who handles supplies with ease, she has feelings for Bright beyond mere respect and turns shy as a girl around him. She died using the La Vie en Rose as a shield to protect him.
#84 Milly Childer | Milly
A mechanic of the La Vie en Rose. She later boards the Argama, sometimes piloting the Mega Rider.
#85 Mashymre Cello | Mashymre | Kenyu Horiuchi
A Neo Zeon officer. He chases the Argama from Shangri-La and sorties himself, but loses to Judau again and again. Utterly loyal to Haman, he treasures the rose she gave him with his orders, coated for keeping. Removed from the front after repeated failures, he is made a Cyber Newtype with his loyalty amplified. His mind grows unstable, and he dies half berserk fighting Glemy's Rakan unit.
#86 Lance Gylen | Lance | Kosuke Meguro
A Neo Zeon Royal Guard. Assigned to watch over the Cyber Newtype Chara, he flanks her with his twin Nee in a Gazu-L.
#87 Nee Gylen | Nee | Bin Shimada
A Neo Zeon Royal Guard. Assigned to watch over the Cyber Newtype Chara, he flanks her with his twin Lance in a Gazu-R.
#88 Gottn Goh | Gottn | Koji Totani
A Neo Zeon officer who serves as aide to Mashymre and Chara, and pilots MS himself. A realist who laments serving one odd commander after another and frets about demotion. But he is ruthless too; on the Moon he used Cecilia, a childhood friend of Torres, to try to blow up the Argama. Ashamed of her weakness, Cecilia sacrificed herself, and Gottn died by his own bomb.
#89 Arius Moma | Arius | Shinya Otaki
Leader of the Neo Zeon transport unit that delivered the Psyco Gundam Mk-II to Glemy. He fights at the front in a Bawoo. Beaten by Judau's group, he is open-minded enough to admit their Newtype power.
#90 Rakan Dahkaran | Rakan | Ryunosuke Obayashi
A battle-hardened Neo Zeon officer. Bold and ambitious, so sure he will never be shot down that he skips his normal suit. He sides with Glemy for rule of Earth once Glemy wins, and his Space Wolf unit harries the Gundam Team. At Axis, the ZZ's high mega cannon wrecks his machine and he retreats.
#91 Glemy Toto | Glemy | Tsutomu Kashiwakura
A Neo Zeon officer. After fighting as a pilot under Mashymre, Haman gives him a unit of his own. He later claims Zabi blood and makes himself the figurehead of a plot to topple Haman and restore the Zabis. At first a sheltered boy smitten with Roux, as his ambition grew he showed his cold, ruthless side, raising a Newtype unit and winning over officers like Rakan. In the end he rides in Ple Two's Quin Mantha and nearly wipes out the Gundam Team. But as he tries to stop Ple Two from going to Judau, Roux's Zeta shoots him dead.
#92 Illia Pazom | Illia | Kimie Sawaki
A Neo Zeon officer assigned as aide to the Cyber Newtype Mashymre, to watch him. A Newtype herself, she flies a Rigelg, and briefly connects with Judau in battle.
#93 Chara Soon | Chara | Hazuki Kadoma
A Neo Zeon officer who gets wildly excited in a mobile suit, fighting Judau's group in the R-Jarja. Her reckless style once got her captured by the Argama, where she lived with Judau's group. Back in Neo Zeon she was enhanced and led Haman's guard. She died half berserk in a Geymalk, protecting Haman from Glemy's Newtype unit.
#94 August Guidan | August | Koji Totani
A Neo Zeon officer. Once captain of the cruiser Mindora, Haman made him Glemy's aide to watch him.
An ambitious man, he joined Glemy's plot once he learned of it. He was shot down by Judau in the Hyaku Shiki.
